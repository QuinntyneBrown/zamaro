# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Zamaro is a production platform where churches book Christian praise and worship
artists in Toronto and the surrounding area. These requirements cover both sides of
the marketplace: churches who book artists ("bookers"), artists who lead worship, and
the Zamaro team who run the platform ("administrators").

The mocks in `docs/mocks/` (every page, dialog and notification, in every state)
set the visual and behavioural direction. The business rules below follow those
mocks: a 25% deposit, an 8% platform fee taken from the artist's payout, and free
cancellation up to 14 days before the event.

## L1-001: Service area
The system shall serve churches and artists located within a reasonable driving
distance of Toronto, and shall only match an artist to an event the artist is willing
to drive to.

## L1-002: Discover available artists
The system shall let anyone find worship artists who are free on a chosen event date
and will travel to the event's location, and shall let them narrow and order those
results.

## L1-003: Artist profiles
The system shall present each approved artist on a public profile page showing their
bio, images, videos, the songs they lead, their availability, their price, and
reviews from churches.

## L1-004: Booker accounts and churches
The system shall let a church representative create an account, describe their church,
and manage the church details used in booking requests.

## L1-005: Saved artists
The system shall let a signed-in booker save artists to a personal shortlist and come
back to it later.

## L1-006: Booking requests
The system shall let a signed-in booker request to book an artist for a specific date
and event, and shall let the artist accept or decline that request.

## L1-007: Payments and payouts
The system shall collect a deposit when a booking is confirmed and the balance after
the event, and shall pay artists their earnings minus the platform fee.

## L1-008: Cancellations and refunds
The system shall let either party cancel a booking and shall apply a published,
predictable refund policy.

## L1-009: Booking messages
The system shall let a booker and an artist exchange messages about a specific
booking without exposing their private contact details before the booking is
confirmed.

## L1-010: Artist onboarding and vetting
The system shall let worship artists apply to join, and shall only publish an artist
after the Zamaro team has vetted them.

## L1-011: Artist profile management
The system shall let an approved artist manage their own profile content, media,
setlist, price and travel radius.

## L1-012: Artist availability
The system shall let an artist manage which dates they are free, and shall keep
availability consistent with confirmed bookings.

## L1-013: Reviews
The system shall let churches review an artist after a completed booking, and shall
show those reviews and an overall rating on the artist's profile.

## L1-014: Notifications
The system shall tell bookers and artists by email when something happens that needs
their attention or changes their booking.

## L1-015: Administration
The system shall give the Zamaro team the tools to vet artists, moderate content,
resolve booking and payment problems, and audit what happened.

## L1-016: Security
The system shall protect accounts, personal data, payments and content against
unauthorised access and the OWASP Top 10 risks.

## L1-017: Privacy and compliance
The system shall handle personal information in line with Canadian privacy and
anti-spam law and shall give people control over their own data.

## L1-018: Performance
The system shall feel fast on mobile networks and shall stay fast under expected peak
load.

## L1-019: Reliability and operations
The system shall stay available, recover from failure without losing bookings or
payments, and give the team the visibility to run it in production.

## L1-020: Responsive design
The system shall work on every screen size from small phones to large desktops.

## L1-021: Accessibility
The system shall be usable by people with disabilities and shall conform to WCAG 2.2
Level AA.

## L1-022: Visual themes
The system shall offer a light theme and a dark theme that both meet the accessibility
requirements.

## L1-023: Graceful loading, empty and error states
The system shall tell people clearly what is happening while content loads, when there
is nothing to show, and when something fails, and shall never lose what they entered.

## L1-024: Localisation
The system shall present dates, times, distances and money the way people in Ontario
expect.

## L1-025: Discoverability and sharing
The system shall make public artist profiles findable by search engines and attractive
when shared as links.

## Assumptions to confirm

These decisions were made to keep the L2 requirements testable. Change them here and
in `L2.md` together.

- The service area is 200 km by road from Toronto City Hall, matching the largest
  radius option in the mocks (L2-001).
- Distances are road distances from a routing provider, not straight lines (L2-002).
- Discover features one headliner: the artist with the most bookings confirmed this
  season among those free on the date, within travel range and matching the active
  filters. It stays first whatever
  the sort; the other results follow the sort (L2-006, L2-007).
- Artists have one booking per calendar day (L2-032).
- Artists respond within 72 hours (24 hours inside 7 days of the event), and bookers
  pay the deposit within 48 hours of acceptance (L2-030, L2-037).
- The deposit is held until the event. The balance is charged 48 hours after the
  event unless the church reports a problem (L2-038, L2-039).
- A church that cancels inside 14 days loses the deposit. An artist who cancels
  refunds everything (L2-042, L2-043).
- Youth events only match artists with a verified Police Vulnerable Sector Check
  from the last 3 years (L2-005, L2-049).
- Artists upload video files rather than linking YouTube or Vimeo (L2-052).
- The request form on the book page (`/artists/{slug}/book`) asks for a service
  start time as well as the fields on the profile's booking stub. It drives
  reminders and when a booking counts as completed (L2-028).
- Layout changes at the L2 breakpoints (SM 576 px, MD 768 px, LG 992 px, XL 1200 px),
  which the design system's `--layout-breakpoint-*` tokens and the mocks share. The
  booking stub is pinned from LG (L2-019).
- Data is hosted in Canadian regions (L2-084).
