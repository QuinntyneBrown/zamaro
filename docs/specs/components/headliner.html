Responsive

Real viewports: each frame is an iframe at that width, so the 768 and 992 px breakpoints apply. The skeleton sits under the headliner in each frame so their heights can be compared.

Measured with the design-system styles: Abigail’s headliner is 934, 549 and 558 px tall at 360, 768 and 1280 px; the skeleton 924, 552 and 580 px. AC-15

Not renderable here: AC-19 (keyboard activation), AC-31 (reduced motion), AC-33 (perf-test measurement).
