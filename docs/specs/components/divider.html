Sizes

One weight per variant: hairline and vertical use --border-width-hairline, strong and perforated --border-width-thick. Width is the container's; a vertical rule takes the row's height.

States

Dividers are not interactive: no hover, focus, disabled or busy state, and Tab never stops on one. What changes is the surface under them. AC-7

Responsive

The divider does not change across breakpoints. These real viewports show that rules keep the container's width at 320 px, and that the label wraps rather than clips: the 160 px frame is a 320 px phone at 200 % zoom.
