Audit filters, page head, preview

From / To in a form grid; “Who’s free on” beside a button; the disabled profile-preview stub date. AC-9, AC-16

Quick-pick presets

A labelled group of small chips after the box; the chip matching the value is pressed; none when no preset matches; disabled while read-only. AC-5, AC-13

Two dates for a retreat

Two date pickers; the page sets “Last day” min to “First day”’s value. Side by side from 768 px. AC-21

Standalone

Without a field the box carries aria-label. AC-17, AC-18

Sizes

36, 44 and 56 px. Small uses 14 px text and is for wide screens only. AC-4

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state; the rest use real attributes. “Open” is the browser’s calendar and cannot be rendered here.
