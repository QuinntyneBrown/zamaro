Variants

Plain text, 1 to 2,000 characters. Abigail gets an email at most every 15 minutes.
Default: three rows, maxlength="2000", vertical resize only; the label and help are the field’s. AC-1, AC-3, AC-14
234 / 2,000
Auto-grow (.textarea--autogrow): grows with Naomi’s 234-character message where the browser supports field-sizing, capped at 20 rem; elsewhere it keeps the 6 rem box and its handle. The counter is the field’s, fed by the reported length. AC-4, AC-12, AC-13, AC-20, AC-29
