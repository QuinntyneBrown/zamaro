Responsive

Artwork has no breakpoints of its own; it fills its column and keeps its ratio. These frames check that nothing overflows at phone widths and that the photo grid goes from two to four columns (the page’s grid).

Not renderable here: AC-9 (rendition widths), AC-10 (fetch priority), AC-12 (layout shift when a photo arrives), AC-17 (tab order), AC-22 (no animation), AC-23 (perf-test measurement).
