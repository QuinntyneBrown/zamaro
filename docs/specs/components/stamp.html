Variants

The eight L2-029 statuses in lifecycle order, then an outcome with no status. Each prints its word and the ink square, so colour never carries the status alone.

A stamp has no pointer or focus states. Its states are the statuses; the last column is the skeleton the page shows in its place while loading.

Responsive

The stamp itself never changes size; the frames show it in the layouts that move around it. Each frame is a real viewport.

AC-21 (reduced motion: nothing animates) and AC-22 (perf-test scenarios) are not renderable here.
