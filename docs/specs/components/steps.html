Responsive

Real viewports: each frame is an iframe at that width, so the steps breakpoint at 992 px and the stepper's scrolling apply.

At 360 px the stepper keeps one line and scrolls inside itself; in the 360 frame the current step (fourth) is shown scrolled into view only once the component's scroll runs, so here it sits at the end of the row. AC-28, AC-29: motion and measurement, not renderable here
