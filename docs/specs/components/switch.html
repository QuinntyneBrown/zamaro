Label at the start, in a settings list

Preferences

Dark theme on.

.switch--start fills the row with the track at its end. Descriptions are the accessible description, not part of the name. The disabled row says why. The status line is the page’s, not the switch’s. AC-2, AC-3, AC-6, AC-10, AC-12

Sizes

One size: a 3 rem × 24 px track in a row at least 24 px tall, 44 px under a coarse pointer. AC-16

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state on the row; the focus cells add the ring’s gap to the track inline, the look the component builds (D-9). Disabled uses the real disabled attribute. There is no busy or invalid state: a failed save is shown by the page flipping the switch back.
