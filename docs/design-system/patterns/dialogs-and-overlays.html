Pattern

Dialogs & overlays

Overlays interrupt, so Zamaro uses them only when a church must decide something before going on: money moving or a booking ending. Everything else stays on the page.

Overview

The mocks deliberately keep booking on the page: the request form is the ticket stub beside the profile, not a pop-up, so the church can scroll the videos and setlist while writing. Overlays are kept for four jobs:

  • Dialog: a decision that moves money or ends something. “Lock in Sat 14 Nov”, “Cancel your booking with Abigail?”.
  • Full-screen dialog: the same dialog on a phone. Below 576px (XS) every dialog fills the screen, with its title and close button fixed at the top (L2-099).
  • Navigation drawer: the compact header’s menu (.dialog--drawer), full height from the left; and the anchored account menu (.dialog--menu) under the avatar.
  • Menu: a short list of actions or places tied to one control. The account menu behind Naomi’s avatar.
  • Tooltip: the name of an icon-only control, on hover and focus. Never essential information.

Composes: dialog, menu, tooltip, button, description list, alert, avatar, toast and the booking ticket.

Stacking

LayerTokenValueRule
Sticky top bar--z-stickyAbove the page; below every overlay.
Menu--z-dropdownCloses when anything else opens.
Backdrop--z-overlayOne backdrop, owned by the open dialog.
Dialog--z-modalOne at a time. A second decision replaces the dialog’s content as a step; never a dialog on a dialog.
Popover--z-popoverOnly inside a dialog (a native select’s list, a date picker).
Toast--z-toastNew toasts wait while a dialog is open; a problem with the dialog’s own action is an alert inside it.
Tooltip--z-tooltipAlways on top, never interactive.

A native <dialog> opened with showModal() sits in the browser’s top layer and makes the rest of the page inert; the tokens order everything that is not in the top layer.

Responsive behaviour

  • Dialogs are centred from 576px, 32rem wide (24rem small, 44rem large), and never taller than the screen minus 32px; the body scrolls, the header and footer stay.
  • Below 576px (XS) they fill the screen: full width and height, no frame, header with title and close fixed at the top, body scrolling, footer sticky above env(safe-area-inset-bottom), buttons equal width (L2-099). The navigation drawer keeps its 20rem width and the account menu stays anchored under the avatar.
  • Menus stay anchored to their trigger at every size and flip to open upwards near the bottom of the screen. The phone’s main navigation is a disclosure panel, not a menu (see navigation).
  • Tooltips do not appear on touch. Icon-only controls on phones need a name that does not depend on the tooltip (aria-labelledby still points at its text).
  • Zoom to 400% turns every dialog into the full-screen layout, so it reflows without sideways scrolling.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Dialogs are native <dialog> opened with showModal(), labelled by their title and described by their first paragraph (APG Dialog (Modal)). Destructive confirmations use role="alertdialog" (APG Alert Dialog). The account menu follows the APG Menu Button pattern. Tooltips follow the APG Tooltip pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles through the dialog’s controls only; the page behind is inert.
EscCloses the dialog (same as “Not now” or “Keep booking”), the menu or the tooltip. Ignored while a payment is being charged.
Enter / Space / ↓On the avatar: opens the menu and focuses the first item.
↑ ↓ / Home EndMoves between menu items; letters jump to the item that starts with them.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field, or to the safest action when there is none: “Not now” for payment, “Keep booking” for cancelling. On close, focus returns to the control that opened the dialog. Menus focus their first item on open and return focus to the trigger on Esc. Tooltips never take focus.

Labelling

Every dialog has a visible title that names the decision, and an icon-only Close with aria-label="Close". The menu trigger has aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and a name that says whose menu it is. A tooltip that names an icon button is that button’s label (aria-labelledby); one that adds a hint is its description (aria-describedby).

Motion

Dialogs rise 16px and fade in over --duration-slow (260ms); tooltips fade over --duration-fast after a 300ms delay. Under reduced motion both appear at once. The backdrop never animates.

Touch

Footer buttons are 44px tall and, on phones, half the screen wide each. Close is 36px, and 44px under a coarse pointer. Menu items are 44px tall. Nothing is reachable only by hover: the tooltip text is also the button’s name.

Content

  • Titles name the decision. Confirmations are a question with the object: “Cancel your booking with Abigail?”. Payments name the outcome: “Lock in Sat 14 Nov”.
  • Body gives the consequences in dollars and dates: “Your $162.50 deposit goes back to your card.” One or two sentences.
  • Buttons repeat the verb: “Cancel booking” and “Keep booking”, “Pay $162.50 deposit” and “Not now”. Never “Yes”, “No”, “OK” or “Confirm”.
  • Menus use nouns for places (“Your requests”) and verbs for actions (“Sign out”), with counts in brackets: “Saved artists (3)”.
  • Tooltips are a short label in sentence case without a full stop: “Share Abigail’s profile”.
