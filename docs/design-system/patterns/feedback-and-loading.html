Pattern

Feedback & loading

Every action gets an answer where the person is already looking. Small results show inline, quick confirmations get a toast, failures get an alert in place of the missing content, and only real decisions get a dialog.

Overview

A worship coordinator often books on a phone between rehearsals. They need to know, without hunting, whether the lineup is still loading, whether a save worked and whether a request went out. Three rules cover it:

  • Answer in place. The feedback appears next to the thing that changed: under the field, on the toggle, in the lineup’s slot. A toast is the exception, for confirmations that need no attention.
  • Hold the shape. While content loads, a skeleton in the final layout holds its place, so nothing jumps when “ABIGAIL MENSAH” lands.
  • Trust, then confirm. Saving an artist is instant and reversible, so the toggle flips at once and the server catches up. If it fails, the toggle flips back and says so.

Composes: inline message, toast, alert, dialog, skeleton, spinner, progress bar, save toggle, button and the ticket card. Longer-lived messages are in notifications; empty and failed regions in empty and error states.

Timing

  • 0–300 ms: show nothing new; keep the old content.
  • 300 ms–8 s: skeleton or busy control, plus a status line: “Finding who’s free on Saturday 14 November 2026…”.
  • After 8 s: the status line changes to “Still checking — thanks for waiting.”
  • On timeout or failure: replace the skeleton with the alert. Keep the search, filters and anything typed.

Optimistic save with Undo

Saving an artist is the most frequent action and is always reversible, so it does not wait for the server.

  1. The person presses the heart on Luz Viva’s ticket. The toggle fills yellow at once (aria-pressed="true") and its name changes to “Remove Luz Viva from your saved artists”.
  2. The Saved count in the top bar goes from 2 to 3.
  3. A toast confirms “Saved Luz Viva” with Undo. Undo puts both back exactly as they were.
  4. If the server has not answered after 1 s, the heart pulses (aria-busy="true").
  5. If the server fails, the toggle and count flip back and a danger toast says “Couldn’t save Luz Viva” with Try again. It stays until dismissed.

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeletons use the same layout classes as the content (.lineup, .ticket, .artist-poster, .profile-layout), so they reflow exactly like it: one ticket column on phones, two from 992px, the stub beside the body from 576px.
  • Toasts sit bottom right, 24rem wide, from 576px; on phones they span the width at the bottom, above the safe area. They never cover the booking stub’s submit while it is focused.
  • Alert actions sit under the text on phones and beside it from 576px. “Try again” comes first.
  • The status line sits right under the section head, so on a phone it is visible without scrolling past skeletons.
  • Busy buttons keep their width at every size; the spinner sits inside the existing padding.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Loading regions carry aria-busy="true"; their skeletons are aria-hidden="true". One role="status" line per region announces progress politely. Failures use role="alert". Toasts live in a polite role="status" region that exists in the page before any toast is added, so screen readers pick up the change. Determinate progress uses role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow. The save toggle follows the APG Button pattern with aria-pressed; alerts follow the APG Alert pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Space / EnterOn a save toggle: saves or removes. Pressing it again is the same as Undo.
TabReaches “Try again” in an alert straight after the section head and filters.
EscDismisses a toast while focus is inside it, and returns focus to the control that caused it.

Focus

Feedback never steals focus. Toasts and inline messages are announced, not focused. After “Show the lineup”, focus stays on the submit; the result count is announced. An alert that replaces a region is announced through role="alert", and its “Try again” is the next stop in the tab order. Undo in a toast is a shortcut, not the only way back: the toggle itself undoes.

Labelling

Status copy says what is loading, with the date: “Finding who’s free on Saturday 14 November 2026…”. Save toggles name the artist and the result of pressing: “Save Elijah Park to your saved artists”. The count badge says “3 saved artists”, not “3”. A toast’s dismiss button names the toast: “Dismiss: Saved Luz Viva”.

Skeletons are decorative: they carry no information and are hidden from assistive technology, so their fills are exempt from 1.4.11.

Motion

The skeleton shimmer sweeps slowly (--duration-deliberate) and stops under prefers-reduced-motion. Toasts slide in over --duration-slow; with reduced motion they appear at once. Spinners and the busy heart hold still too (L2-103); their busy label or status text says the work is in progress. The indeterminate progress bar stops and shows a half-tone full bar instead.

Touch

The save toggle is 44px round, inside a stub with 16px of padding. Toast actions are 36px buttons in a 16px-padded toast. Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Loading: present tense, says what and for when: “Checking calendars…”, “Finding who’s free on Saturday 14 November 2026…”. Use an ellipsis character (…).
  • Slow: one reassuring line, no blame: “Still checking — thanks for waiting.”
  • Confirmation: past-tense verb and the name: “Saved Luz Viva”, “Removed Elijah Park”, “Request sent to Abigail”. No exclamation marks.
  • Undo is always the single word “Undo”.
  • Errors: what happened, what is safe, what to do: “We couldn’t load who’s free on Saturday 14 November 2026. Your date, location and filters are kept.” + “Try again”.
  • Counts: numerals and the noun: “7 artists free”, “3 saved artists”.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Skeleton widths and heights were inline styles in the loading mocks. They are now .skeleton--short/medium/long/portrait/wide/control/control-lg/target/figure.
  • Stage skeletons read --palette-ink-800/700. They now use --color-bg-stage-raised and --color-bg-stage-raised-hover.
  • Skeleton heights 0.9rem and 1.8rem are now 1rem and 1.75rem, on the spacing grid.
  • The toast region sat at bottom: --space-16 to clear the mock bar, and no toast existed although the note promises one. There is now a .toast component and the region sits at --space-6 (phones --space-4 plus the safe area).
  • .alert was danger-only by default. It now has tones .alert--danger|success|warning (info by default); the mocks use alert alert--danger.
  • Disabled chips in the loading mock had no disabled style. .chip:disabled now greys them out.
  • The pressed “Saved” button on the profile had no pressed style. It now fills yellow with a filled heart.
