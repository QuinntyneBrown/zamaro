Pattern

Empty & error states

When the lineup is empty or something breaks, say what happened in plain words, keep everything the church already chose, and offer the next best step, usually another date.

Overview

There are three different reasons a part of the page has nothing to show, and each gets a different treatment:

  • First run: there is nothing yet. Miriam Haile is new, so she has no videos and no reviews. The section stays, with a quiet note and one thing the church can do now.
  • No results: the search worked but nobody matches. Every gospel choir near Burlington is booked on Christmas Eve. Explain why, then offer nearby dates and a wider radius, each with a count.
  • Error: we could not load it. Say it plainly, say that the search and any sent request are safe, and offer Try again plus a way out.

The empty states carry a rotated stamp, like a rubber stamp across a gig poster: “Sold out”, “Fresh on the bill”, “Opening night”, “Show postponed”. The stamps are a fixed list, so they stay recognisable.

Composes: empty state, error page, alert, button, chip, poster hero, breadcrumb, tour dates and spinner.

Recovery actions

  • Date swap: up to three nearby dates with the number of artists free, respecting the current filters. Picking one updates the date field, the poster headline and the lineup.
  • Widen the radius: one primary button with the radius; the count goes in the sentence above it.
  • Clear a filter: “Show all styles” releases the pressed chips and keeps the date and place.
  • Try again: re-runs exactly the same search. After a third failure, add “Check status.zamaro.ca”.
  • Way out: every error offers a second action that does not depend on the failing service: an email link or the lineup.

Structure

An empty state is a dashed ticket: stamp, title, one or two sentences, a recovery block and actions. An error is an alert in the same slot.

Responsive behaviour

  • Empty states and alerts take the full width of the slot they replace. The .empty padding drops from 40px to 24px in the quiet variant, which is the one used inside narrow profile sections.
  • The date swap is an auto-fill grid with a 9rem minimum: three across on a laptop, two on a 360px phone, the third wrapping below.
  • Action buttons wrap with .cluster. Button labels stay short enough for 360px: the count in the button is the bare number, “Search within 120 km · 2 free” (L2-011), and the sentence above says what is free, not “Search within 120 km · 2 choirs free”.
  • Alert actions sit under the text on phones and beside the icon column from 576px.
  • Page errors keep the top bar, the breadcrumb and the footer, so a phone user can always get back to the lineup without the browser’s Back.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A no-results state that replaces results after a search is a role="status" container, so its title is announced politely. First-run empties are ordinary content with no role. Errors use role="alert" (APG Alert pattern), added to the page when the failure happens, not on load. Date-swap entries are native buttons.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the nearby dates, then the primary action, then the secondary, in reading order.
Enter / SpacePicks a nearby date or runs the action. Picking a date re-runs the search.

Focus

Focus does not jump to an empty state; it is announced. After a nearby date is picked, focus moves to the lineup heading so the new results are read from the top. A page error moves focus to its <h1> (“This profile didn’t load”) on load, like any new page.

Labelling

The stamp is real text, read before the title. It never carries information the title lacks. Date-swap buttons read as “Sun 20 Dec, 2 choirs free”. The struck-through name in .error-poster is decoration with aria-hidden="true", so the poster reads “Show cancelled” and the sentence below names the artist.

Motion

The stamp is rotated 3 degrees and does not move. Date-swap buttons lift on hover over --duration-fast; reduced motion makes the change instant. Nothing in an error animates.

Touch

Date-swap buttons are at least 9rem wide and two lines tall, well over 44px. Actions are medium buttons, 44px tall, 8px apart.

  • Never “Oops”, “Uh-oh”, emoji or exclamation marks. Never blame the church.
  • Name the date and the place: “Christmas Eve within 40 km”, not “for your search”.
  • One or two short sentences. The stamp is never more than three words.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • .alert was danger-only by default. It now has tones .alert--danger|success|warning with info as the default; the mocks now use alert alert--danger.
  • The artist error used an inline padding-block on its container. It is now .page-error.
  • The empty profile’s About copy used style="margin-top: var(--space-4)". It is now .section__body.
  • The mock’s primary empty-state action “Search within 120 km · 2 choirs free” was too long to fit at 360px. It is now “Search within 120 km · 2 free” (L2-011).
