Pattern

Data tables & lists

Most Zamaro collections are a lineup of tickets, because churches choose artists by face, place and price. Use a table only when people compare numbers across rows and act on them, like their own booking requests.

Overview

A search for Sat 14 Nov within 120 km of Burlington returns seven artists. That is a short list of very different things (a soloist, a 30-voice choir, a piano trio) and the church decides by looking. So the lineup is a set of admission tickets: artwork, name, style, distance and the price on the stub, the headliner first. Tables appear later, in the church’s own records, where rows are alike and numbers matter.

  • Filter with chips that toggle on and off, and sort with one native select. Both re-order in place and keep the date.
  • Load a page, then show more. The lineup loads 24 artists and “Show more artists” brings the rest (L2-010); profile reviews show 4, then 10 more at a time (L2-018). Numbered pages only for records people return to.
  • Tables scroll inside their frame on phones, with the first column pinned, unless they are four columns or fewer and can become a list.

Composes: ticket card, headliner, chip, select, list, tour dates, setlist, table, pagination, badge, avatar, save toggle and skeleton.

Focus moves to the first new item; the button goes when nothing is left.

Filtering and sorting

  • Chips are toggle buttons in a <fieldset class="filters"> with a legend. Styles combine with “or” (Band or Gospel choir); price combines with “and”. Each chip shows how many artists it would match; a style that would match no one is disabled. With Under $800 on, that is Abigail Mensah ($650), Elijah Park ($350) and Daniel & Ruth Okonkwo ($700): Solo vocalist 2, Hymns 2, Acoustic 2, and no Band or Gospel choir.
  • The sort is a native <select> in a .field--inline: “Closest first” (default), “Highest rated”, “Price, low to high”. Changing it re-orders the lineup in place; focus stays on the select.
  • The count in the section kicker updates and is announced: “Sat 14 Nov · 3 free · within 120 km”.
  • Filters and sort are part of the search, so they live in the URL and survive Back (see navigation).
  • When filters rule everyone out, the empty state offers “Show all styles”.

Responsive behaviour

  • Lineup: one ticket per row on phones and tablets, two from 992px. Below 576px the price stub moves under the body, with the perforation across.
  • Section head: the sort sits beside the title when there is room and wraps under it on phones. Chips wrap onto as many lines as they need; they never scroll sideways.
  • Tables with five columns or more scroll inside .table-wrap; the first column (.table__first, the artist) stays pinned so each row keeps its name. The page itself never scrolls sideways.
  • Tables with four columns or fewer become a list below 768px: each row a .list__item with the first column as the title and the rest as meta.
  • Pagination wraps; the status line goes above the page links on phones.
  • Tour dates keep three columns down to 360px; long notes wrap under the date.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The lineup is an <ol role="list"> (its order is the sort) with an aria-label that names the date. Each ticket has one link, on the name, stretched over the card; the save toggle sits above it. Chips are toggle buttons (APG Button) in a fieldset with a legend. Tables use <caption>, scope on headers and a row header per row; sortable columns follow the APG Sortable Table example with a button in the header and aria-sort on the <th>. Pagination is a <nav> with aria-current="page".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSort, each chip, then per ticket: the name link, then the save toggle.
Space / EnterToggles a chip; sorts by a column (again to reverse); activates “Show more artists”.
↑ ↓Changes the sort select; the lineup re-orders without moving focus.
Shift+Scroll or arrows in the table frameScrolls a wide table sideways; the frame is focusable (tabindex="0") when it overflows.

Focus

Filtering and sorting keep focus where it is. “Show more artists” moves focus to the first new ticket’s link. After a column sort, focus stays on the header button. A ticket lifts on :focus-within so a keyboard user sees which card the link belongs to.

Labelling

Chip names include their count with a hidden noun: “Under $800, 3 artists”. Row actions repeat the row’s name: “Pay deposit for Abigail Mensah, Sat 14 Nov”, with the visible label first (WCAG 2.5.3). The empty actions header is labelled “Actions” for screen readers. Result counts are announced through role="status".

Motion

Tickets lift and tilt slightly on hover and focus over --duration-base; under reduced motion they change shadow without moving. Re-sorting does not animate the order.

Touch

Chips are 44px tall with 8px between them. Page links are 44px squares. The whole ticket is the link target, and the save toggle is a separate 44px circle in the stub. Small “Pick” buttons sit in 44px-plus rows.

Content

  • Column headers are one or two words: “Artist”, “Date”, “Status”, “Fee”. No units in cells when the header has them.
  • Numbers are tabular and right-aligned: “$1,800”, “$650”. Distances read “74 km from you”.
  • Status words are a fixed set, with a badge tone each: Waiting (info), Accepted (success), Booked, Played, Declined, Cancelled. Never “Pending approval”.
  • Sort options say the order: “Closest first”, “Highest rated”, “Price, low to high”.
  • Chip labels are styles and limits people use: “Gospel choir”, “Hymns”, “Spanish”, “Under $800”.
  • Counts use numerals: “Showing 24 of 30”, “Page 1 of 2 · 23 requests”.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The sort field used style="min-width: 12rem" in four mocks. It is now .field--inline.
  • Disabled chips (discover/loading) had no disabled style. .chip:disabled now greys them out.
  • .chip__count used opacity: .75, a contrast risk. It now uses the full text colour.
  • Pressed chips borrowed --color-fg-default and --color-bg-canvas. They now use the inverse pair.
  • Ticket notches always painted --color-bg-canvas; they now read --ticket-notch-bg, so tickets on a card show the right colour. The notch grew from 0.9rem to 1rem.
  • Tables are in the admin mocks; numbered pagination is still not in any mock. The lists that grow load more in place instead: “Show 10 more” on pages/artist-reviews/default (L2-018) and “Show older entries” on pages/admin-audit/default; “Show more artists” (L2-010) is not drawn because the mocked lineup has seven artists. The guidance here said “Show everything up to 12, then Show 6 more”; it now follows L2-010’s 24-per-page and the button copy in the specs. Booker and artist lists use .booking-list rows rather than tables.
