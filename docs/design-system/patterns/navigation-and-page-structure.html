Pattern

Navigation & page structure

Every Zamaro page is laid out like a gig poster: the charcoal top bar, a poster hero that names the date or the artist, a marquee strip, paper sections, and the stage footer. The search travels with you from page to page.

Overview

Zamaro is small: find who is free, look at an artist, ask to book. The structure keeps those three steps one click apart and never makes a church re-enter its date.

  • The shell is the same on every page: skip link, top bar on the stage, <main>, footer on the stage. Only the poster and the sections change.
  • The poster is the page title. Its <h1> is the date on Discover (“Who’s free Sat 14 Nov”) and the artist’s name on a profile.
  • The search is state, kept in the URL. Date, place, kind of gathering, radius, filters and sort travel to the profile and back, through the breadcrumb or the browser’s Back.
  • The phone gets the same places, behind a menu button in the top bar.

Composes: skip link, top bar, poster hero, marquee, container & stack, breadcrumb, footer, band, badge, avatar, menu, tabs, sidebar navigation and the booking ticket.

  1. Skip link to #main, the first thing in the page.

Responsive behaviour

  • Top bar: below 1024px the primary links hide behind the menu button and Saved shows only the heart and the count. From 1024px the links and the word “Saved” show and the menu button hides. The bar is sticky at every size.
  • Poster: one column on phones and tablets; from 1024px the Discover poster puts the headline and the booking bar side by side, and the profile poster puts the artwork to the right of the name.
  • Marquee: a single clipped line at every size; it never wraps or scrolls on its own.
  • Profile layout: from 1024px the stub sits in a 22rem column and stays pinned under the top bar. Below 1024px it follows the reviews, and “Book for Sat 14 Nov” jumps to it.
  • Breadcrumb: wraps onto a second line rather than truncating; it only ever has two items.
  • Footer: one column on phones, three from 768px, four from 1024px.
  • Sidebar navigation (account pages): a column from 1024px; below that, the same links appear at the top of the page as a list, and tabs scroll sideways if they overflow.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Landmarks in this order: skip link, <header> with <nav aria-label="Primary">, <main id="main">, <footer>. One <h1> per page, in the poster; each section has an <h2>, tickets and headliner <h3>. The breadcrumb is <nav aria-label="Breadcrumb"> with aria-current="page" on the last item (APG Breadcrumb). The phone menu follows the APG Disclosure pattern (aria-expanded + aria-controls), not the Menu pattern. The avatar opens a real account menu. Account tabs follow the APG Tabs pattern with manual activation.

Focus

On the stage the focus ring is yellow (--color-accent-on-stage). The sticky top bar never covers the focused element: scroll-padding-top is the bar height plus 16px (WCAG 2.4.11). After Back, focus returns to the ticket link the person opened. A newly loaded page starts at the top with focus on the document; the skip link is the first stop.

Labelling

Each <nav> has a distinct label: “Primary”, “Breadcrumb”, “Account”. The menu button’s name follows its state (“Open menu” / “Close menu”). Saved is “Saved artists, 3” when only the icon shows. The avatar is “Account menu for Naomi Fraser”. Page titles carry the search: “Who’s free Sat 14 Nov · Zamaro”, “Abigail Mensah · Zamaro”.

Motion

The shell does not move. In-page jumps scroll smoothly, and instantly under prefers-reduced-motion. The marquee is static.

Touch

Top-bar links, the Saved button and phone-menu items are 44px tall (--target-comfortable); the menu button and avatar are 44px and 40px squares with 12px between them. Breadcrumb links are at least 24px tall.

Content

  • Navigation labels are short nouns or the task: “Discover”, “How booking works”, “For artists”, “Saved”.
  • The breadcrumb names the search, not just the page: “Discover · Sat 14 Nov”. The current item is the artist’s name.
  • Poster kickers set the scene in one line: “Worship artists within driving distance of Toronto”, “Headliner · Gospel & contemporary vocalist”.
  • Section kickers carry the live summary: “Sat 14 Nov · 7 free · within 120 km”.
  • Page titles: the page’s point, then “· Zamaro”.
  • Marquee copy repeats what is on the page (towns, song titles) and is never the only place something appears.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The top-bar “Saved” button combined .nav-link, .btn--ghost and an inline style reset. .nav-link now resets buttons itself.
  • .brand-mark was 1.9rem (30.4px, off the grid). It is now 2rem.
  • The brand mark, marquee, band, count badge and skip link read primitives (--palette-ink, --palette-paper*). They now use --color-fg-on-accent, --color-border-on-accent and stage tokens.
  • Badge count min-width was 1.4em; it is now 1.5em.
  • Tabs, sidebar navigation and the phone menu panel are not in the mocks yet; they are part of the core set for the account pages and phones.
