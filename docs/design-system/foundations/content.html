Content

Warm, plain and practical, like a worship leader talking to a church office. The poster vocabulary adds personality in a few words; the rest is clear.

Overview

Bookers are coordinators and volunteers planning a service. They want to know who is free, what it costs, and whether it is safe to commit. Copy answers those questions in one or two short sentences per block.

Voice and tone

Warm“Seven artists are free that Saturday and will drive to Burlington.”“7 results match your query.”

The gig-poster words (lineup, headliner, setlist, on tour, sold out, the bill) stay in headings, kickers and stamps. Sentences that explain money, dates or errors use plain words.

Casing and punctuation

  • Write sentence case in the source. CSS uppercases display type, buttons, labels and kickers, so screen readers read words, not shouted letters.
  • Curly apostrophes and quotes (’ “ ”), an ellipsis character (…) and a middle dot (·) to separate details: “Sat 14 Nov · 7 free · within 120 km”.
  • No exclamation marks, except a review that has one.
  • Ampersand only in names and fixed pairs (“Gospel & contemporary”).

Numbers, dates, money, distance

These follow L2-110 (Ontario, en-CA, America/Toronto); the product formats them in one service, never by hand, and the patterns come from the translation catalogue (L2-111).

Buttons and links

  • Buttons start with a verb and name the outcome: “Request to book”, “Try again”, “Ask Miriam for a recording”.
  • Links name the destination: “See Abigail’s profile”, “Back to the lineup”.
  • Destructive actions name the loss: “Cancel booking”, “Remove from saved”.
  • No “Click here”, “OK”, “Submit” or “Learn more” on its own.

Error formula

What happened (in a few words, sometimes in the poster voice) + reassurance (what is safe) + what to do (one action).

We lost the signal

We couldn’t load who’s free on Saturday 14 November 2026. Your date, location and filters are kept.

Field errors name the field and the fix: “Pick a date after today”, not “Invalid date”.

Empty-state formula

Stamp (two or three poster words) + title (what is empty, specifically) + one sentence (why, or what will happen) + one action (the most likely next step), plus alternatives when the system knows them (nearby free dates).

  • “Sold out” · “Nobody’s free Christmas Eve within 40 km” · “Every gospel choir near Burlington is booked for Christmas Eve. Try a nearby date, or widen the radius.” · Search within 120 km · 2 free
  • “Fresh on the bill” · “No videos yet” · “Miriam is filming her first live set this month.” · Ask Miriam for a recording
