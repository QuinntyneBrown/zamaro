Foundation

Motion

Motion is physical and quick: things lift off the paper, press back flat, or snap like a stamp. Nothing drifts or loops for decoration.

90–260msReduced motion in tokens

Overview

Motion confirms an action or shows where something came from. Durations are short (90–260ms). The one bouncy curve is reserved for the save toggle’s snap.

Easing

--duration-slow · --ease-standard
--duration-slow · --ease-enter
--duration-slow · --ease-exit
--duration-slow · --ease-spring
  • Standard: anything that changes in place.
  • Enter: things arriving (dialogs, toasts) decelerate into place.
  • Exit: things leaving accelerate away.
  • Spring: overshoots, like a rubber stamp. Only for the save toggle and avatar.

Choreography

  • Hover: lift 4px up and left, shadow appears (fast). Press: flat, no shadow (fast).
  • Ticket hover: lift plus a 0.4° counter-clockwise tilt (base).
  • Save: scale 1.08 on hover, 0.94 on press, spring easing; filled heart when on.
  • Dialog: fades in and rises 16px (slow, enter). Toast: rises 16px (slow, enter) and slides right when leaving (base, exit).
  • Skeletons sweep left to right once every 900ms. Spinners turn once every 900ms.
  • The marquee does not scroll. A moving strip would be the only animation on the page that the booker didn’t ask for.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, tokens.css sets fast, base, slow and deliberate to 0.01ms, so every transition and keyframe in components.css finishes instantly with no extra media queries. Skeletons stop sweeping and the indeterminate progress bar becomes a static half-tint. Spinners and the busy heart stop too (L2-103): components.css sets animation: none on them, and the busy label or status text beside them says that work is in progress. --duration-loop is not zeroed, because a near-zero infinite loop would spin at full speed. Smooth scrolling is turned off.
