Foundation

Shape

Square and printed. Corners are cut, not rounded. Weight comes from rules: 1px hairlines, 2px for anything you can touch, 4px poster frames.

Square corners1 / 2 / 4px rules

Overview

A screen print has hard edges. Every container, control and ticket has square corners (all radius tokens are 0). The scale is kept so a partner theme could round things without touching components. Only circles that mean something are round.

The round exceptions

NFLoading
Avatars (people are round), the save toggle (a badge pinned to a ticket), radios (shape signals the control type), spinners, and the ticket’s perforation notches.

Rule weights

--border-width-hairline
--border-width-thick
--border-width-poster
  • Hairline (1px) in --color-border-default: setlist and tour-date dividers and badges. Decorative.
  • Thick (2px) in --color-border-strong: every control, ticket, card, alert and stub. Dashed thick rules are perforations: the ticket stub, the stub head, empty states.
  • Poster (4px): the headliner frame, the yellow rules under the top bar and over the footer, the band frame, the current-tab underline.

Usage

  • Use var(--radius-md) in components even though it is 0: a theme can then change it.
  • Dashed means tear-off. Use it only where something is a stub or a placeholder.
  • Never mix a rounded and a square control in one row.
