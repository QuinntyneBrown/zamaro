Accessibility

Church bookers include volunteers in their seventies on old phones. Every component meets WCAG 2.2 AA by construction, and the checkers enforce what they can.

Overview

Accessibility is built into the tokens (contrast, focus, targets, motion) and documented on every component page in the same seven parts. This page lists the commitments and how to test them.

Focus and targets

  • Every focusable element gets a two-tone ring on :focus-visible only (L2-101): a --focus-ring-width outline in --color-focus-ring at --focus-ring-offset, with the gap filled by --color-focus-ring-offset (yellow inside, ink outside on newsprint; charcoal inside, yellow outside in the dark theme and on the stage). scroll-padding-top keeps a focused element clear of the sticky top bar.
  • Text fields: --shadow-focus, a double ring (yellow inside, ink outside on newsprint; charcoal inside, yellow outside on the stage), so the ring shows against both the field and the page.
  • Targets: --target-min (24px) is the floor; --target-comfortable (44px) for anything tapped often.

Component contract

Every component page has an #accessibility section with the same seven parts:

  1. Role and pattern: the native element or the WAI-ARIA APG pattern, linked.
  2. Keyboard: a table of keys and effects.
  3. Focus: where focus starts, where it goes, what the ring looks like.
  4. Labelling: what must be named, how, and what is announced.
  5. Contrast: the token pairs with live ratios in both themes.
  6. Motion: what moves and what reduced motion does.
  7. Touch: target sizes and hover alternatives.

Testing

  • check_contrast.py tokens/tokens.css: every declared pair, both themes.
  • check_design_system.py docs/design-system: labels, names, alt text, landmarks, one h1, links, no raw colours.
  • Tab through each component page’s examples; check order and visible focus.
  • Screen reader pass on the booking stub (form), a dialog and the lineup (list of tickets) with VoiceOver and NVDA.
  • Zoom to 200% and 400%; emulate prefers-reduced-motion, prefers-contrast: more and forced-colors.
  • Screenshots at 360 and 1280px in both themes.
