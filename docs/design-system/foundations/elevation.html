Foundation

Elevation

Nothing glows on paper. Raised things cast a hard, offset shadow, like a second colour printed a few millimetres out of register: ink on newsprint, yellow on the stage.

No blur4 levelsYellow shadows on stage

Overview

Zamaro has four shadow levels and no blur. A shadow is always offset down and to the right by a fixed amount (--size-offset-*), drawn in --color-shadow. On hover, interactive surfaces lift up and to the left by the same offset (--transform-lift), so the shadow appears to grow from under them.

Surface hierarchy

Surfaces are flat colour planes separated by rules. Elevation comes from shadows, not from tints.

Sunken: wells, table heads
Canvas: the page
Surface: tickets, inputs, cards
Raised: dialogs, menus
Stage: top bar, hero, footer
Sunken: wells, table heads
Canvas: the page
Surface: tickets, inputs, cards
Raised: dialogs, menus
Stage: top bar, hero, footer

In the dark theme surfaces get lighter as they rise (sunken #141413 → canvas #1b1b19 → surface #232320 → raised #2d2d2a), and the shadow turns yellow so lifted things read as lit from the stage. The stage itself sinks below the canvas (#141413) so the poster still feels like a separate printed block.

The hover lift

No. 05

Elijah Park

Buttons lift by --transform-lift over --shadow-1. Tickets also tilt by 0.4° over --shadow-2, like a ticket picked up off the table.

Accessibility

  • Elevation is never the only cue: raised surfaces also have a 2px rule (at least 3:1), so they read in forced-colours mode where shadows disappear.
  • The lift is decorative and uses --duration-fast, which reduced motion zeroes.
