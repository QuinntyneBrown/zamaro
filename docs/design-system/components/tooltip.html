Tooltip

A small ink label that appears beside an icon-only control when you point at it or tab to it: “Share profile” over the share arrow, “Save to compare later” over the heart.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Tooltip · role="tooltip"Class .tooltip

Overview

Use a tooltip to show, in words, what an icon-only control does: the share button on a profile, the round save toggle on a ticket, the heart in the compact top bar. It shows on hover and on keyboard focus, after a short delay, and never holds anything you can click.

  • Above is the default. The tooltip sits 8px over the control with a small ink arrow.
  • Below (.tooltip--below) is for controls near the top of the screen, such as the top bar.
  • With a shortcut: a <kbd> after the text, where a keyboard shortcut exists.

Do not use a tooltip for information people need to complete a task (put it on the page as help text), for errors (use an inline message), on controls that already have a visible label, or on touch-only screens where nothing hovers.

Anatomy

  1. LabelBold 12px sentence case (--text-caption), paper on ink, 4 × 8px padding, up to 16rem wide.
  2. Shortcut (optional)A <kbd> in the mono overline style with a hairline box.
  3. ArrowA 4px triangle in the same ink, centred on the control.
  4. Anchor.tooltip-anchor wraps the control and the tooltip; the control points to the tooltip with aria-describedby.

Variants

Hover or tab to the controls; the first one in each example is shown open with data-state="open".

Below (in the top bar)

Your saved artists Menu
.tooltip--below: the bar sits at the top of the viewport, so tooltips open downwards. On the stage (and in the top bar) the tooltip turns into a paper label with a hairline outline, so it never sinks into the charcoal behind it.

Sizes

Tooltips have one size. Long text wraps at 16rem; if it needs more, it belongs on the page.

States

Hidden is the default. Real pages open the tooltip from :hover and :focus-within on the anchor; documentation forces it with data-state="open".

  • Opening waits --duration-tooltip-delay (300ms) of hover or focus, then fades in over --duration-fast, so sweeping the pointer across a row of tickets does not flash a tooltip on each.
  • Closing is immediate when the pointer leaves or focus moves on.
  • Static (data-state="open") shows without the delay or transition. Documentation only.

Responsive behaviour

  • The tooltip is centred on its control and grows to fit its text up to 16rem, then wraps. It does not move to stay on screen, so do not put above-tooltips on controls within 8rem of the left or right edge; on phones, prefer labels on screen.
  • Touch screens have no hover. The tooltip still shows on keyboard focus, but a tap fires the button first, so on phones the control must make sense without its tooltip: icon-only buttons always carry an aria-label, and important ones get a visible label instead.
  • Use .tooltip--below inside the top bar and within 3rem of the top of the viewport.

Theming

The tooltip reads the inverse pair: --color-bg-inverse for the label and arrow and --color-fg-inverse for the text. On newsprint it is a charcoal label with paper text; on the stage theme the pair flips to a paper label with charcoal text, so it always stands out from the surface around it. It has no component tokens. It stacks at --z-tooltip, above dialogs, toasts and the sticky top bar.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Tooltip pattern: the tooltip has role="tooltip" and an id; the control points to it with aria-describedby. The control’s name comes from its own aria-label, which names the object (“Share Abigail Mensah’s profile”); the tooltip adds a short description and any shortcut, which is also declared with aria-keyshortcuts. A tooltip never receives focus and never contains links or buttons.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the control opens its tooltip after the delay; moving focus closes it.
EscCloses the open tooltip without moving focus (WCAG 1.4.13 Dismissible), through the data-state="closed" hook; see below.
Shortcut (e.g. S)Runs the control’s action when the page defines that shortcut; it must not fire while typing in a field.

Focus

Focus stays on the control. The label sits 8px away, just beyond the 3px ring and its 3px offset, so the ring never covers the text; only the arrow’s tip meets it.

Labelling

Keep the visible tooltip text consistent with the accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3): “Share profile” matches “Share Abigail Mensah’s profile”. When a toggle changes state, update the tooltip text with it (“Save to compare later” → “Saved · press to remove”).

WCAG 1.4.13 (Content on Hover or Focus) is met in components.css: the tooltip takes pointer events and an invisible bridge (.tooltip::before) spans the 8px gap, so the pointer can move onto it without closing it (hoverable); data-state="closed" hides it until the pointer or focus leaves, so Esc can dismiss it.

Motion

A fade only, over --duration-fast, after the hover-intent delay. Under prefers-reduced-motion the fade drops to near zero; the 300ms delay stays, because it prevents flicker rather than decorating.

Touch

Tooltips do not help on touch; the controls they describe are 44px targets (.save, .btn--icon) that work without them. Nothing important may live only in a tooltip.

Content

  • Two to four words, sentence case, no full stop: “Share profile”, “Save to compare later”, “Your saved artists”.
  • Start with the verb the control performs; do not repeat the artist’s name, which is already on the ticket.
  • Show the state for toggles: “Saved · press to remove”.
  • Shortcuts are single keys in a <kbd>, written as the key shows on a keyboard: S, not “s”.

// Esc dismisses (WCAG 1.4.13) through the data-state="closed" rule:
document.addEventListener('keydown', (e) => {
  if (e.key !== 'Escape') return;
  document.querySelectorAll('.tooltip-anchor:is(:hover, :focus-within) > .tooltip')
    .forEach((tip) => tip.setAttribute('data-state', 'closed'));
});
