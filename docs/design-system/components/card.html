Card

A framed paper panel that holds one thing: a booking request, a package, a saved artist. It has a 2px charcoal frame, square corners and a perforated footer, and it lifts with a hard shadow when the whole card is a link.

Overview

Use a card when several items of the same kind sit side by side in a card grid and each needs its own small layout: a title, a few facts, an action. Use a plain list when items are one line each.

Zamaro already has specialised cards for its signature moments. Use them, not .card, for these jobs:

  • An artist in search results is a ticket, with a perforated price stub and notches.
  • The top result is the headliner, framed with a 4px rule.
  • A church’s quote is a review, with the yellow quote mark.
  • The booking form is the booking ticket.

The generic card covers everything else: request and booking summaries in a booker’s account, packages an artist offers, saved artists, dashboard panels.

Anatomy

  1. Media (optional).card__media: a halftone artwork or photo, full bleed, with a 2px rule under it.
  2. Title.card__title: a heading in the display face, 28px, uppercase. Holds the card’s one link when the card is interactive.
  3. Header aside (optional)A status stamp, a badge or a save toggle, aligned to the top right of .card__header.
  4. Body.card__body: facts and short copy, 12px between items, 24px side padding.
  5. Footer (optional).card__footer: a dashed tear line, then price and actions, pushed to the bottom so footers line up in a row.
  6. Frame2px --card-border on --card-bg, square corners. No shadow at rest.

Panel

.panel is the card the signed-in pages are built from: a paper frame with a .panel__head (an h2/h3 in .panel__title plus an optional caption, code or action on the right) over a dashed rule. It does not lift or link as a whole; actions inside it are ordinary buttons. .panel--raised adds the 8px hard shadow for the one panel that holds the page’s primary action, such as the payment aside on a booking.

Message

.message is one note in a booking’s thread: a sunken block with a 4px yellow rule on the left, a mono .message__meta line (“You · Fri 9 Oct, 10:15 a.m.”) and the text, quoted in a <blockquote> when it is the church’s own words. Messages sit in a panel and stack 16px apart, oldest first.

Stat

.stat is a small figure card for the artist dashboard: an overline .stat__label, the figure in .stat__value (tabular, h2 size) and a caption .stat__hint that says what is behind the number, often with a link. .stat--accent fills the one stat that needs action (requests awaiting reply) with yellow. .stat-grid lays them out two across, four from 992px. Sizes

Cards have one padding scale (24px sides). Their width comes from the grid they sit in, not from a size modifier.

States

Hover is forced with data-state="hover" on the card; focus is shown on the title link, which the card reacts to through :focus-within.

  • Hover and focus-within (interactive only) lift the card 4px up and left over a hard 6px shadow: ink on newsprint, yellow on the stage theme.
  • Selected doubles the frame in --color-border-on-accent, lays the yellow highlighter behind the card and adds a 4px print shadow. Pair it with a checked radio, checkbox or aria-current; the style alone says nothing to a screen reader.
  • Loading: render the card frame with skeleton lines inside and aria-busy="true" on the list, not a spinner per card.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill the column they are given. In .grid--cards they are at least 288px wide, so one per row on phones, two or three on larger screens.
  • The footer stays at the bottom (margin-top: auto), so cards of different heights in one row line up their prices and actions.
  • Footer content wraps: on a narrow card the action moves under the price rather than shrinking.
  • Media keeps its aspect ratio (16:9 with .art--wide); never crop faces with a fixed height.
  • .stat-grid: two columns on phones and tablets, four from 992px (--layout-breakpoint-lg).
  • Panels fill their column; in a detail layout the raised aside sits beside the main panels from 992px.

Theming

The card has two component tokens: --card-bg (defaults to --color-bg-surface) and --card-border (defaults to --color-border-strong). .card--flat sets --card-border to --color-border-default; .card--selected sets it to --color-border-on-accent and paints --color-accent-subtle behind. The media rule and the footer’s tear line also read --card-border, so a re-skin is two variables.

In the dark theme the card is a dim charcoal panel with a light frame, and the hover shadow turns yellow (--color-shadow). On the yellow band or the stage, wrap the card in data-theme="light" so it stays a paper panel, as the booking ticket does.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

No ARIA role. Use <article> for a self-contained item (a booking), <li> inside a list of cards, or <label> around a radio for a choosable card. An interactive card is not a link itself: it contains one real link (in the title) whose hit area is stretched over the card, which keeps the link’s name short and lets other buttons inside work. This follows the inclusive “block link” approach; there is no APG pattern for it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the title link, then to each button inside the card, in DOM order.
EnterOn the title link: opens the item. On a button: its own action.
SpaceOn a button or the radio of a choosable card: activates it.
↑ / ↓In a group of choosable cards: moves the radio selection (native radio behaviour).

Focus

The ring is drawn on the title link; the whole card lifts too (:focus-within), which makes the focused card easy to spot in a grid. Buttons inside sit above the stretched link (z-index: var(--z-raised)) so they can receive their own clicks and focus.

Labelling

Label the card by its title (aria-labelledby). The title link’s name is the item’s name alone (“Elijah Park”), not the whole card’s text. Icon buttons inside name their object: “Remove Elijah Park from your saved artists”. Media needs alt or aria-label that describes the picture, not “photo”.

The flat card’s hairline frame is under 3:1 and is decoration; flat cards are never interactive, so the frame does not need to identify a control.

Motion

The lift and shadow take --duration-base (160ms) with --ease-standard. Under prefers-reduced-motion they change instantly; the shadow still appears, so the hover cue remains.

Touch

The stretched link makes the whole card a large target. Keep buttons inside at least 36px tall with 8px around them, so a tap meant for the card does not hit the save toggle, and the other way round.

Content

  • The title is the thing’s name: an artist, a booking code, a package. Keep it to one or two lines.
  • Body facts follow the order a booker scans: date, kind of gathering, place. Separate them with a middle dot.
  • Money in the footer, with the right word: “From $900” for a starting price, “$650” for an agreed one, “Balance $487.50 after” for what is left.
  • One status per card at most: a booking’s stamp (“Confirmed”, “Requested”) or another state’s badge.

Sources

The generic .card is not in the mocks; it stays ready for summaries that link as a whole. The mocks use its relatives, which share its frame and rules:

  • .panel and .panel--raised: pages/booking-detail/default and every booking status, pages/request-detail/default, pages/apply/default (“After you apply”), pages/dashboard/default, pages/earnings/default

  • .stat: pages/dashboard/default, empty, loading
  • Tickets, the headliner and review cards: pages/discover/default, pages/artist/default

Drift fixed during extraction: the card was written to the corrected tokens from the start (hover shadow offset 6px via --shadow-2, --color-shadow instead of --shadow-color, --transform-lift instead of --lift). pages/dashboard/loading sized its stat skeletons with inline style="width: …"; they now use the skeleton modifiers (.skeleton--short/medium/long).
