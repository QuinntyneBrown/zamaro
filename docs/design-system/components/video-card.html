Video card

Bookers want to see an artist lead a room before they book. A video card is a wide thumbnail with a yellow play stamp showing the running time; pressing it plays the clip.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <button>Class .video

Overview

Use video cards in the “Watch her lead” section of a profile, inside a .media-grid. Each card is one <button>: pressing it plays the clip in place, in the card’s 16:9 frame, as a native <video controls> with captions when the artist supplied them (L2-014). It never leaves the page and never autoplays.

  • The first card is the featured clip and spans the full row from 768px.
  • For still photos use the photo grid; for a link to an external channel use a link.
  • An artist with no videos keeps the section with a quiet empty state (“No videos yet”).

Anatomy

  1. Thumbnail.art.art--wide: 16:9 halftone or a still from the clip.
  2. Play stamp.video__play: yellow label with an ink rule, pinned 16px from the bottom-left. “Play · 6:12”.
  3. Caption.video__caption: song and where it was filmed, 14px muted, outside the button.
  4. Button.video: an unstyled full-width <button>; the whole thumbnail is the target.

Variants

One component; the grid position and the thumbnail vary.

Sizes

Video cards have one size: they fill their grid cell at 16:9. The featured card is the same component spanning the row.

States

Hover and active are forced with data-state; focus with data-state="focus" on the button. Real pages get them from :hover, :active, :focus-visible and aria-busy.

While the profile loads, a .skeleton--wide holds each card’s place. If the player fails, an inline message under the card says so and the button tries again. With no videos, the section shows a quiet empty state.

Both themes

Twi praise medley — Harvest Sunday 2025
Twi praise medley — Harvest Sunday 2025
  • Hover lifts the play stamp 4px up and left over a hard ink shadow; the thumbnail does not move.
  • Active presses the stamp flat.
  • Focus draws the ring around the whole thumbnail.
  • Busy (aria-busy="true" with aria-disabled="true", like every busy button) adds a spinner to the stamp while the player loads. The button keeps its label.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under --layout-breakpoint-md (768px), cards stack one per row, full width.
  • From 768px, the first card spans the row and the rest sit three across.
  • The stamp stays the same size at every width; on a third-width card it still fits “Play · 12:45”.
  • The player takes the card’s place at the card’s size, so playing a clip never moves the grid.

Theming

The stamp reads --color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent and --color-border-on-accent; its hover shadow is --color-border-on-accent, so it prints ink in both themes. The thumbnail follows artwork tokens, and the caption reads --color-fg-muted.

No component tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button type="button"> (APG Button) that swaps itself for a native <video controls> player in the same frame. Only phrasing elements (<span>) go inside the button. The caption is a sibling, outside the button.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabOne stop per video.
Enter / SpaceStarts the clip in place and moves focus to the player, whose native controls take over.

Focus

The 3px ring wraps the whole 16:9 thumbnail, with a 3px offset so it is visible against both the halftone and the page.

Labelling

Children of a button are presentational, so the thumbnail’s own label is not read. The button’s aria-label carries the song, where it was filmed and the duration in words: “Play Way Maker, live at Bethel Pentecostal, Hamilton, 6 minutes 12 seconds”. It starts with “Play”, the visible word on the stamp, so voice users can say “click Play” (WCAG 2.5.3). The busy button keeps the same name; the player announces its own status. A failure message is linked with aria-describedby.

Motion

The stamp lift takes --duration-fast and drops to instant under reduced motion. Videos never autoplay, and thumbnails never animate on hover. The busy spinner keeps turning because it reports status.

Touch

The whole thumbnail is the target: at least 180px tall at full width on a phone. Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Stamp: “Play · ” and the duration as m:ss. Write it in sentence case; CSS uppercases it.
  • Caption: song title, an em dash, then the setting: “Way Maker — live at Bethel Pentecostal, Hamilton”.
  • Label: “Play {song}, {setting}, {m} minutes {s} seconds”.
  • Order: the clip that best shows the artist leading a congregation goes first, not the newest.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The stamp read --palette-ink for its label, rule and hover shadow. It now reads --color-fg-on-accent and --color-border-on-accent.
  • The hover read --offset-1 and --lift. It now reads --size-offset-1 and --transform-lift, and pressing flattens it.
  • No busy state existed. aria-busy="true" now adds a spinner to the stamp.
  • The mock typed “PLAY” in capitals. Source copy is now sentence case (“Play · 6:12”); CSS uppercases it.
  • The wide skeleton used an inline aspect-ratio. It now uses .skeleton--wide.
