Calendar

The artist’s tour-dates sheet: a month grid where every day reads Free, Unavailable, Requested (with a count) or Booked, and where Free and Unavailable days can be selected and changed together from a bulk bar.

Overview

The calendar is how an artist sees and sets their availability for the next 18 months, one month at a time (L2-056). Each day carries one status: Free (the default for a new artist), Unavailable (a weekly default or a range the artist blocked), Requested with the number of open requests, or Booked once a booking is Confirmed. Booked days cannot be changed here (L2-057). In the app it is a zm-* component in the shared components library used by the zamaro app, and one rendered month (a .calendar with its grid of day cells, as on pages/availability) is the “availability calendar month” composite, which has its own perf-test scenario (AGENTS.md, component perf tests).

  • Use the calendar where a person manages many dates at once. Select days, then act on them from the bulk bar: “Mark unavailable” (or “Mark free” when the selection holds Unavailable days) or “Clear”.
  • Use the date picker to choose one date in a form (the booker’s event date, the start and end of a blocked range).
  • Use tour dates for a read-only list of an artist’s next dates on their profile.

Anatomy

  1. Head.calendar__head: the month as an h2-sized heading (.calendar__month) with previous/next month buttons.
  2. Legend.calendar__legend: one .calendar__swatch per status, each with its word.
  3. Weekday header.calendar__weekday: charcoal stage strip, mono overline; the full day name is in visually hidden text.
  4. Day.calendar__cell (the gridcell) holds a .calendar__day button with the number (.calendar__num) and, from MD, an event line (.calendar__event); below MD a square .calendar__dot marks a day that has one.
  5. Status filldata-status on the day: free (paper), blocked (hatched), requested (yellow), booked (ink), outside or past (sunken, muted).

Sizes

The calendar has one size: it fills its container in seven equal columns. Days are at least 44px tall (--layout-calendar-cell) and 77px from MD.

  • Hover tints Free and Unavailable days yellow-subtle and deepens Requested; Booked days don’t react.
  • Focus draws the two-tone ring inside the day (negative offset) so it is not clipped by the grid lines.
  • Selected is aria-selected="true" on the gridcell, drawn as the focus-ring shadow on the day so it reads in every status.
  • Today (aria-current="date" on the day) underlines the number.
  • Loading: the frame, legend and weekday strip render at once; each day holds a short .skeleton and the grid is aria-hidden while the page is aria-busy="true" (availability/loading).
  • Empty is a calendar of Free days, never a blank grid (L2-056; availability/empty). Error replaces the grid with an alert and Try again (availability/error).

Responsive behaviour

  • Seven equal columns at every width (minmax(0, 1fr)), so the grid never scrolls sideways; at 320px each day is about 40px wide and 44px tall.
  • Below MD (768px): days show the number and, when something is on, a small square dot. The event text is hidden; it stays in the button’s accessible name.
  • From MD (768px): days grow to 1.75 × 44px and show the event line (“Requested · 1”, “Harvest Point”, “Studio”) instead of the dot, truncated with an ellipsis.
  • The head wraps: the month and the arrows stay together; the legend drops to its own line on phones.
  • The bulk bar wraps its actions under the count on phones. With .bulk-bar--sticky it stays 16px above the bottom of the viewport while the month scrolls.

Theming

The calendar has no component tokens; each status maps to semantic tokens. Free is --color-bg-surface; Unavailable hatches --color-bg-surface-sunken with --color-border-default; Requested is --color-accent with an inner --color-fg-on-accent rule; Booked inverts to --color-fg-default behind --color-bg-canvas text; outside and past days use --color-bg-surface-sunken and --color-fg-disabled. Grid lines are the --color-border-strong showing through 2px gaps, and the weekday strip is the stage. In the dark theme Booked becomes a paper day on the charcoal sheet; Requested stays yellow. The bulk bar uses the inverse pair (--color-bg-inverse, --color-fg-inverse) with a yellow frame when sticky.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An APG grid with aria-multiselectable="true", labelled by the month heading. Rows are role="row"; weekday headers are columnheaders; each day is a gridcell that carries aria-selected and holds a <button>. Days outside the month and past days are not buttons and are hidden from assistive technology. Booked days are buttons with aria-disabled="true" so they stay discoverable and say why they can’t change.

Focus

The focused day shows the two-tone ring inset into the cell and is raised above its neighbours. Selection uses the same ring as a shadow, so a focused selected day is unmistakable; the bulk bar’s count changes as the selection changes.

Labelling

Each day button’s name is the full short date, the status word and the detail: “Sat 14 Nov, Requested · 1, Riverside Community Church”, “Mon 16 Nov, Unavailable, weekly default”, “Sun 1 Nov, Booked, Harvest Point Church. Booked dates can’t be changed”. The visible number is aria-hidden. Weekday headers show “Sun” and give “Sunday” in visually hidden text. The legend is a labelled list, so status is never conveyed by colour or pattern alone. The bulk bar is a labelled region whose count is a polite role="status".

Outside and past days use --color-fg-disabled; they are inert and hidden from assistive technology, so 1.4.3 does not apply. The hatch on Unavailable days is decoration on top of a measured background.

Motion

None beyond colour changes on hover. Month changes swap the grid without sliding.

Touch

Every day is at least 44px tall and about 40px wide at 320px, with 2px between days; drag-to-select is not used, so selection is always a tap per day or a range from the dialog.

Content

  • The four status words are fixed by L2-056: Free, Unavailable, Requested, Booked. Requested always carries its count: “Requested · 2”.
  • The event line is the shortest useful name: the church’s first words for Booked (“Harvest Point”), the artist’s own note for Unavailable (“Studio”), “Weekly” for the weekly default.
  • Month heading: “November 2026”. Arrow names say where they go: “Previous month, October 2026”.
  • Bulk bar: count, then the range in the L2-110 short pattern: “2 days selected · Sat 28 – Sun 29 Nov”. Actions are verbs: “Mark unavailable”, “Clear”.

Drift noted during documentation:

  • The stylesheet still selects .calendar__day[aria-selected="true"] from before selection moved to the gridcell; the cell rule is the one to use.
  • Neither data-status="past" nor aria-current="date" appears in the November mocks.
  • The loading mock sized its day-number skeletons with the --space-5 token inline. They now use .skeleton--day.
