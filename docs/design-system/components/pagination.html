Pagination

Pagination splits a long list into pages and says where you are in it: “Showing 1–20 of 63”. Page numbers are set in the poster face, like seat numbers on a ticket.

Overview

Use pagination under a list of records that people scan, compare and come back to once it is too long for one screen: the administrator’s bookings and artists tables, a church’s past bookings, an artist’s past requests. Twenty records per page. Each page has its own URL (?page=2), so the back button and shared links work.

  • Numbered is the default: previous, page numbers, next, and a status line on the left.
  • Previous / next only fits short, ordered sets where the page number means little, such as an artist’s past bookings in the dashboard.
  • With a gap (.pagination__gap) keeps the first, last and nearby pages when there are more than five.

Lists that are browsed once load more in place instead of paging (see Data tables and lists): the lineup loads 24 artists and “Show more artists” appends the rest (L2-010), profile reviews show 4 and then 10 more at a time (L2-018), and the audit log’s “Show older entries” follows a cursor. Never use infinite scroll: it hides the footer and loses the person’s place.

Anatomy

  1. Status.pagination__status: mono stub type, muted, “Showing 1–20 of 63”.
  2. PreviousA chevron link labelled “Previous page”. On page 1 it is aria-disabled="true" with no href.
  3. Current pagearia-current="page": solid ink square with paper numerals.
  4. Page link44px square, 2px charcoal frame, numerals in the 22px display face.
  5. NextA chevron link labelled “Next page”.

Sizes

Pagination has one size: 44px squares, which meet the comfortable target on every screen.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state. Current and disabled come from aria-current="page" and aria-disabled="true".

  • Hover fills the square with the yellow highlighter. Squares do not lift, so a row of them stays calm.
  • Current is solid ink (paper on the dark theme) and stays a link, so reloading the page is possible.
  • Disabled previous or next turns grey on a subtle fill and loses its href, so it is skipped by Tab. Keep it in place so the row does not jump.
  • Loading: after a page link is followed, the list area takes aria-busy="true" and shows skeleton tickets; the pagination itself does not change until the new page arrives.

Responsive behaviour

  • The status and the list sit on one row with space between them and wrap onto two rows when they do not fit (flex-wrap). At 360px the status goes above the numbers.
  • Below 576px show at most five numbered squares: first, current, the pages either side, and last, with gaps. Seven squares plus previous and next fit in 320px.
  • Never scroll the pagination sideways and never shrink the squares below 44px.

Theming

Squares read --color-bg-surface, --color-fg-default and --color-border-strong; hover reads --color-accent-subtle; the current page uses the inverse pair --color-bg-inverse / --color-fg-inverse; disabled uses --color-fg-disabled on --color-bg-subtle; the status uses --color-fg-muted. There are no component tokens.

On the dark theme the frames turn light, the current square becomes paper with charcoal numerals, and the hover highlighter becomes dim amber. Pagination only appears on paper, never on the stage.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav> landmark with a specific label (“Booking pages”, “Request pages”), and a list of links. There is no APG widget for pagination; it is plain links, so no arrow-key handling is needed. The current page has aria-current="page". Disabled previous or next is an <a> without href plus aria-disabled="true".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through previous, the page numbers and next. Disabled items and gaps are skipped.
EnterFollows the link and loads that page.

Focus

The shared ring around the square. After a new page loads, move focus to the list’s heading (“Bookings”) or the first row, and update the status, so keyboard and screen-reader users start at the new results rather than at the bottom of the page.

Labelling

Number links have aria-label="Page 2", which keeps the visible number at the start of the name. Previous and next are icon-only and labelled “Previous page” and “Next page”. Gaps are aria-hidden. The status line is plain text; when pages load without a full reload, mirror it in a polite role="status" region.

Disabled items are exempt from contrast minimums (WCAG 1.4.3), and they are also skipped by the keyboard.

Motion

None. The hover fill changes instantly. Page changes scroll to the results with the page’s smooth scrolling, which is turned off under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Squares are 44 × 44px (--target-comfortable) with 4px between them; each target is large enough on its own (WCAG 2.5.8).

Content

  • The status says the range and the total with an en dash: “Showing 1–20 of 63”. Add the noun when the list is not obvious: “Showing 61–80 of 140 bookings”.
  • Use plain numerals. Do not write “Page” in the squares.
  • For previous/next only, name the direction in the list’s own terms: “Newer” and “Older” for dated lists.
  • Page size: 20 records, as the data tables and lists pattern sets.

Sources

Not in the mocks yet; part of the core set, and no L2 requirement pages a list yet. The mocked record lists are all short enough for one screen. It is ready for when they grow:

  • The administrator’s tables in pages/admin-bookings/default and pages/admin-artists/default.
  • Past bookings in pages/bookings/past and past requests in pages/requests/default.

The lists that do grow in the mocks load more in place instead, as the specs say: “Show 10 more” on pages/artist-reviews/default (L2-018) and “Show older entries” on pages/admin-audit/default. Earlier versions of this page used the lineup and profile reviews as the examples; L2-010 and L2-018 load those in place, so the examples are now record lists.

Drift fixed during extraction: none, since no mock uses it. The disabled style uses --color-fg-disabled for both text and frame, matching disabled buttons.
