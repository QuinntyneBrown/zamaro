Ticket card

Each artist in the lineup is an admission ticket: halftone art on the left, the name and where they come from in the middle, and a perforated stub with the price and a save toggle.

Overview

Use a ticket for an artist in a list of results: the Discover lineup, saved artists, “three similar artists free on the same date”. The whole ticket opens the artist’s profile; the round save toggle on the stub saves them without leaving the list.

  • The featured artist at the top of Discover (the most-booked artist free that day) is the headliner, not a ticket. Tickets follow it in a .lineup grid in the active sort order, numbered from No. 02.
  • For anything that is not an artist (a booking request, a church), use a card.
  • For a single date on a profile, use tour dates.

In the frontend the ticket is a zm-* component in the shared components library. One ticket card as it sits in the Discover lineup (as on pages/discover/default) is the “artist ticket in the Discover results” composite, which has its own perf-test scenario (AGENTS.md, Component perf tests).

Anatomy

  1. Art.art.ticket__art: halftone placeholder or photo, 6.5rem wide (9rem from 576px), full height, ruled off on the right.
  2. Number and rating.ticket__no: mono overline, muted. “No. 02 · ★ 4.6 · 17 churches”.
  3. Name.ticket__name: an <h3> in --text-h3. Its link stretches over the whole ticket.
  4. Meta.ticket__meta: two short lines: act type and styles (“Band · Hymns”, L2-006), then where and how far.
  5. PerforationA 2px dashed rule between body and stub, with two half-circle notches cut at its ends.
  6. Price.ticket__price: “From” as a mono overline over the amount in --text-figure.
  7. Save toggle.save, raised above the stretched link so it stays clickable.
  8. FramePaper surface, 2px --ticket-border. Hover lifts 4px and tilts −0.4° over a 6px print shadow.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 576px: two columns, art (6.5rem) and body. The stub runs across the bottom with a horizontal perforation; the notches sit on the left and right edges.
  • From --layout-breakpoint-sm (576px): three columns, art (9rem), body and stub (10.5rem). The perforation turns vertical and the notches move to the top and bottom edges.
  • Lineup: one column, then two from --layout-breakpoint-lg (992px), 32px apart.
  • Names wrap; they never truncate. “Grace Tabernacle Mass Choir” takes two lines at 360px.
  • The stub’s price and save toggle stay on one row on phones (price left, toggle right).

Theming

The ticket reads --color-bg-surface, --color-fg-default and --color-fg-muted, plus three component tokens declared on .ticket:

  • --ticket-border (--color-border-strong): frame, art rule, perforation and notch outline. .ticket--booked sets it to --color-border-default.
  • --ticket-notch-bg (--color-bg-canvas): the fill of the two notches, which must match whatever is behind the ticket. Override it on the ticket itself (inline or with a more specific selector) when tickets sit on a surface, in a dialog or on the stage.
  • --notch (1rem): the notch radius.

In the dark theme the ticket is a charcoal surface with a light frame, and the hover shadow turns yellow (--color-shadow). The notches follow the dark canvas automatically.

/* Tickets inside a paper panel */
.panel .ticket { --ticket-notch-bg: var(--color-bg-surface); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each ticket is an <li> in an <ol class="lineup" role="list"> labelled with the date (“More artists free Saturday 14 November 2026”). role="list" restores list semantics that Safari drops when list styling is removed. The ticket has exactly one link, the name, whose ::after covers the whole ticket; the save toggle is a separate APG toggle button raised above it with --z-raised. Never wrap the ticket in an <a>: a button inside a link is invalid and unreachable.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabTwo stops per ticket: the name link, then the save toggle.
EnterOn the name: opens the profile. On the toggle: saves or removes.
SpaceOn the toggle: saves or removes.

Focus

The ring is drawn around the name link (3px, offset 3px); :focus-within also lifts the whole ticket, so keyboard users see the same response as mouse users. The save toggle has its own ring.

Labelling

The link text is the artist’s name, so the list reads as a list of names. The art is role="img" with a description of the photo. The save toggle is named after the artist (“Save Elijah Park to your saved artists”) and exposes aria-pressed; see save toggle. The booked state is text in a badge, not just the muted colour. The price reads “From $350”.

Contrast

Number, meta, booked name
Frame and perforation

Dark: name and price

The booked frame (--color-border-default) is deliberately quiet; the badge text carries the meaning.

Motion

The lift and tilt take --duration-base (160ms). Under prefers-reduced-motion the duration drops to near zero, so the ticket changes position without animating. The saving pulse respects the same setting through the save toggle.

Touch

The whole ticket is the link’s target, and the save toggle is 44px with at least 16px around it, so a tap near the heart never opens the profile by accident. Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Number: position in the lineup, rating and review count: “No. 04 · ★ 5.0 · 6 churches”. An artist with no reviews shows “New” in place of the rating: “No. 05 · New” (L2-006).
  • Name: the artist’s own spelling, “&” for duos.
  • Meta: line one is the act type written as its style name, then the other styles (“Gospel choir”, “Duo · Acoustic, Hymns”, L2-006); line two is where and how far (“Scarborough · 81 km from you”). Distances are whole road kilometres; when the routing provider is down they are estimates and read “about 81 km from you”, and anything under 1 km reads “Under 1 km from you” (L2-002). The headliner uses the same forms without “from you”.
  • Price: “From” plus the lowest rate, no cents: “$2,400”.
  • Booked: say when: “Booked Sun 15 Nov”, not just “Unavailable”.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Ticket notches always painted --color-bg-canvas. They now read the --ticket-notch-bg component token.
  • The notch radius was 0.9rem (off the 4px grid). It is now 1rem.
  • The frame, art rule and perforation read --color-border-strong directly. They now read --ticket-border, which .ticket--booked overrides.
  • The hover read --lift; it is now --transform-lift. The price used an inline display-font shorthand; it now reads --text-figure.
  • Skeleton sizes were inline styles. They now use .skeleton--short, --figure and --target.
  • .ticket--booked is still not used by any mock; it stays ready for similar-artist lists.
