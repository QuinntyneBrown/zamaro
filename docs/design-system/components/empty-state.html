Empty state

A dashed, blank ticket with a rubber stamp across it: “Sold out”, “Fresh on the bill”. It says why there is nothing here and gives the booker one good next move.

Overview

Use an empty state wherever a list or section can come back with nothing in it. It replaces the content in the same place, keeps the controls that led there (the search, the filters), and offers a way out.

  • No results: a search found nobody. Say which part of the search is the problem and offer nearby alternatives, such as other dates with the .date-swap list.
  • Filtered out: the filters hide everyone. Offer to clear the filter that matters.
  • First run: the person hasn’t done this yet (no bookings, no requests, nothing saved). Explain what will appear and how to start.
  • Nothing yet: an artist is new and a section has no content (no videos, no reviews). Use the quiet size so the profile doesn’t look broken.

If the content failed to load, that is an error, not an empty state: use an alert or the error page. While it is still loading, use a skeleton.

Anatomy

  1. Stamp.empty__stamp: a 2px-ruled rubber stamp in the display face, tilted 3°. Two or three words of poster voice. Decorative.
  2. Title.empty__title: what is missing, in plain words. 40px (--text-h2) full, 22px (--text-h4) quiet. Use a heading at the right level.
  3. ExplanationOne or two sentences: why it is empty and what to try.
  4. ActionOne primary button, plus at most one secondary. Date swaps go above the buttons.
  5. ContainerPaper surface with a 2px dashed --color-border-strong rule, like an unused ticket. 40×32px padding full, 24px quiet.

Sizes

Use the full size when the empty state replaces the main content of a page (the lineup). Use the quiet size (.empty--quiet) inside a section of a larger page (videos and reviews on a profile), inside the booking stub, or in a dialog.

Full: 40px top and bottom, 32px sides, 24px gaps, --text-h2 title. Quiet: 24px padding, 16px gaps, --text-h4 title.

States

The empty state itself is static. Its date swaps and buttons are interactive; their pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state. A date swap lifts and fills yellow on hover, like a primary button.

  • Appearing after a search: the empty state replaces the skeletons in a container with role="status", so its title and sentence are announced.
  • Searching again: pressing a date swap or the primary action puts the lineup back into its loading state (skeletons, busy button). The empty state is removed, not hidden.
  • Becoming non-empty: when content arrives (a first review), the empty state is replaced by the list; never show both.

Responsive behaviour

  • The empty state fills its container’s width and stacks its parts in one column at every breakpoint. Text stays left-aligned, like the rest of the poster.
  • Date swaps form a grid of 9rem-minimum cells: one column on narrow phones, two or three across on wider screens.
  • Action buttons wrap onto a new line. A label that is too long wraps inside the button rather than overflowing, which looks broken on a poster button, so keep labels short. The full size’s 32px side padding leaves about 260px at 360px: “Search within 120 km · 2 free” with its arrow wraps to two balanced lines there, and the earlier “Search within 120 km · 2 choirs free” wrapped to three. Below 576px the side padding drops to 20px (--space-5) for that reason.
  • The full size keeps 32px side padding on phones, which leaves about 260px for the title and buttons at 360px. Prefer the quiet size inside narrow columns such as the profile sidebar.

Theming

The container reads --color-bg-surface and a dashed --color-border-strong rule; text is --color-fg-default; the stamp is currentColor. Date swaps are paper buttons with a --color-border-strong rule that turn --color-accent with an ink label, --shadow-1 and --transform-lift on hover. There are no component tokens; re-skin through the semantic tokens.

In the dark theme the empty ticket is a charcoal surface with a light dashed rule and the hovered date swap glows yellow with a yellow hard shadow. The stamp follows the text colour, so it reads as pale ink on the stage.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A plain container. When it appears as the result of something the booker did (a search, a filter), give the container role="status" so the title and sentence are read out, as the discover mock does. When it is part of the page at load (first run, a new artist’s reviews), it needs no role. The title is a real heading at the next level down from its section (<h3> under the lineup’s <h2>). Date swaps are native buttons in a list. No APG widget pattern applies.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the date swaps, then the actions, in DOM order.
Enter / SpaceRuns the search for that date, or activates the action.

Focus

Don’t move focus into the empty state when it appears; the status announcement is enough, and focus stays on the search or filter the booker just used. After a date swap is pressed, focus moves to the lineup heading once results load.

Labelling

The stamp is decoration and repeats the title’s meaning in poster slang (“Sold out”, “Opening night”), so hide it with aria-hidden="true". Each date swap’s name must read naturally: keep a space between the date and the count (<strong>Sun 20 Dec</strong> 2 choirs free) so it isn’t read as “Dec2”.

Motion

The empty state appears without animation. Date swaps lift 4px on hover over --duration-fast; reduced motion removes the movement and keeps the colour change.

Touch

Date swaps are at least 64px tall and 9rem wide, 12px apart. Action buttons are 44px. Hover is never required: every date swap shows its date and count at rest.

Content

  • Title: what isn’t there, with the specifics the booker chose: “Nobody’s free Christmas Eve within 40 km”, “No videos yet”, “No church reviews yet”.
  • Sentence: why, and what to try. “Every gospel choir near Burlington is booked for Christmas Eve. Try a nearby date, or widen the radius.”
  • Action: a verb with the outcome: “Search within 120 km · 2 free” (L2-011), “Be her first booking”, “Remove ‘Hymns’”. One primary.
  • Stamp: two or three words of gig-poster voice (“Sold out”, “Fresh on the bill”, “Opening night”, “Doors open”). Skip it if a pun would sound flippant, such as on a funeral booking.
  • Never blame the booker (“You have no…”) and never leave an empty state without a next step.
