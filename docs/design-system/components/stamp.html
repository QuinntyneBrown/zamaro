Status stamp

A rubber stamp, slightly askew, that says where a booking stands: Requested, Accepted, Confirmed, Completed, or one of the exits Declined, Withdrawn, Expired and Cancelled.

Overview

Every booking carries exactly one status, and the stamp is how that status is printed wherever the booking appears: in Naomi’s bookings list, in Abigail’s requests inbox, on the booking and request pages, in the itinerary of a dialog and in toasts. The words and the order come from the booking lifecycle (L2-029): Requested → Accepted → Confirmed → Completed, with the exits Declined, Withdrawn, Expired and Cancelled.

  • Use a stamp for a booking status and for the one-word outcome of another record (an application “Submitted”, a profile “40%” complete). It is read-only.
  • Every booking status is a stamp, never a badge: .stamp--lg in the page head of one booking, request or admin booking, the default stamp in lists and toasts, and .stamp--flat in table and receipt rows. Use a badge for counts, availability and states outside the booking lifecycle (“3” requests, “Free”, “Booked” on a tour date, an application “Submitted”, a payment “Held”). A held payment is a warning badge beside the booking’s stamp. A badge is a label; a stamp is a verdict.
  • Use an inline message or alert when the status needs a sentence (“Pay the $162.50 deposit by Mon 12 Oct, 3:15 p.m.”). Put the stamp beside it, not instead of it.

Anatomy

  1. Ink square::before, half an em, in the stamp’s colour. A shape cue so the status never depends on colour alone.
  2. Status wordMono overline, uppercase, stamp tracking. One word from L2-029.
  3. Double ruleA 2px border plus a 1px outline 2px outside it, both currentColor; the whole stamp is rotated −2° (−4° large).

Variants

One modifier per status. Colour follows the meaning: the happy path in ink and yellow, the money moment in yellow, refusals in red, lapses in amber, and finished or withdrawn records in muted ink.

Sizes

Requested Requested Confirmed
Default (lists, rows, toasts), .stamp--lg (the page head of one booking or request) and .stamp--flat (inside a receipt row or a panel head, where a tilt would fight the alignment).
  • Default: 12px mono overline, 2px × 8px padding, rotated −2°. In every list row, itinerary and toast.
  • Large (.stamp--lg): the display face at --font-size-2xl, 4px × 12px padding, rotated −4°. Once per page, beside the booking number in the page head.
  • Flat (.stamp--flat): any size, no rotation. Use it in tables, receipts and panel heads.

States

A stamp is not interactive: it has no hover, focus or disabled state. Its states are the statuses themselves, plus loading.

Responsive behaviour

  • A stamp never wraps (white-space: nowrap) and keeps its size at every breakpoint; the longest word, “Withdrawn”, is about 7rem.
  • In a booking list row the stamp sits in the status line under the meta on phones and moves to the right-hand status column from MD (768px).
  • In a page head the large stamp wraps under the title on narrow screens; the head is a flex row that wraps, so nothing overflows at 320px.

Theming

The stamp has no component tokens. Text, square and both rules read currentColor, which each modifier sets from a semantic token: --color-fg-default (Requested), --color-success-fg (Accepted), --color-fg-on-accent on --color-accent (Confirmed), --color-fg-muted (Completed, Withdrawn, Cancelled), --color-danger-fg (Declined) and --color-warning-fg (Expired). The fill is --color-bg-surface, so in the dark theme stamps print light ink on charcoal paper; Confirmed stays yellow in both.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A plain <span> with the status as visible text. No role is needed: the word is the information. When the stamp sits away from the thing it describes (a page head), make sure the heading or row it belongs to comes first in reading order, so it reads “ZAM-0114 … Requested”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the stamp; it is never focusable. Put any action (Pay deposit, Leave a review) in a real button next to it.

Focus

None. The stamp is static text.

Labelling

The word is the label; don’t add aria-label or replace the word with an icon. The ink square and the strike-through on Cancelled are decorative cues, so status never depends on colour alone (1.4.1). When a status changes on screen, announce it through the page’s polite status region or toast.

The default stamp is 12px text, so every status needs the full 4.5:1; the rules are the same colour and so clear the 3:1 non-text minimum.

Motion

None. The tilt is static; nothing animates when a status changes.

Touch

Nothing to tap.

Content

  • Use the L2-029 word exactly, in sentence case in the markup (“Requested”); CSS uppercases it. Never invent statuses such as “Pending”, “Paid” or “Done”.
  • One word. Deadlines, amounts and next steps go in the text next to the stamp: “1 day left · reply by Sat 10 Oct, 3:15 p.m.”.
  • Use the same word for both sides of a booking: Naomi and Abigail both see “Accepted”.
  • Outside bookings, keep to a single past-tense word or a number: “Submitted”, “40%”.

Sources

Introduced by the MVP mocks for booking management; used on these screens:

  • pages/bookings/default, bookings/past: one stamp per row (Confirmed, Requested, Accepted; Completed, Declined, Withdrawn)
  • pages/booking-detail (default, accepted, confirmed, completed, declined, withdrawn, expired, cancelled): the large stamp in the page head
  • pages/requests/default, pages/request-detail and pages/dashboard/default: the artist’s side of the same statuses
  • dialogs/accept-request, cancel-booking, pay-deposit, write-review, withdraw-request: the stamp on the page under the dialog
  • notifications/booking-toast and request-toast: rows behind the toast
  • dialogs/delete-account/blocked: .stamp--flat in a receipt row; pages/edit-profile/empty: a flat “40%”; pages/apply/success: “Submitted”, the applicant’s own verdict in the page head
  • pages/admin-bookings/default and held: .stamp--flat in the status column; pages/admin-booking/default: the booking’s stamp in the page head, with a “Held” warning badge beside it in held

Drift noted during documentation:

  • .stamp--paid and .stamp--done predate the L2-029 lifecycle and are used by no mock; they are kept only as aliases.
  • Some mocks write the modifiers in the other order (stamp--expired stamp--lg on the four expired-link pages, such as pages/verify-email/expired; stamp--confirmed stamp--flat in dialogs/delete-account/blocked); the order does not matter.
