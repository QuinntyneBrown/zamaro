Chip

Chips are the small stamped tags under “Filter by style” on the lineup. Press one to filter, press it again to clear it. The same shape also labels artists and lists the filters in use.

Overview

Chips come in three kinds that look alike but behave differently:

  • Filter chips are toggle buttons (<button aria-pressed>) in a .filters fieldset. They change the lineup at once: “Band”, “Gospel choir”, “Under $800”. Pressed chips turn solid ink with a tick.
  • Static tags (.chip--static) are plain text in a stamp, for facts about an artist: “Hymns”, “Spanish” (the L2-008 style names). Not interactive.
  • Removable chips (.chip--removable) list the filters in use, each with its own remove button.

Use checkboxes for multiple choices in a form that is submitted, radios for one choice, and a badge for status (“Free Sat 14 Nov”). Small chips also serve as the quick-pick cities under “Church location” on Discover (L2-004).

Anatomy

  1. Tick (pressed)A “✓” added by CSS when aria-pressed="true", so pressed never relies on the ink fill alone.
  2. LabelMono stub type, 14px, uppercase, wide tracking. One to three words.
  3. Count (optional).chip__count, regular weight in the label’s colour: how many artists match.
  4. Container44px tall (--chip-height), 16px side padding, 2px charcoal rule, square. Pressed: ink fill, paper text.

Sizes

Medium (44px) is the default for filters, comfortable on a phone. Small (36px) fits the quick-pick cities and dense rows. Static tags are 24px because nobody taps them.

Responsive behaviour

  • Filter chips wrap onto as many rows as they need (.filters is a wrapping flex row with 8px gaps). They never scroll sideways, so no filter is hidden off-screen.
  • Seven chips take two or three rows on a 360px phone. With more than about ten, move filters into a panel with checkbox groups and show the active ones as removable chips.
  • Labels never wrap inside a chip; keep them to three words.

Theming

Chips have four component tokens: --chip-bg (--color-bg-surface), --chip-fg (--color-fg-default), --chip-border (--color-border-strong) and --chip-height (--target-comfortable). Hover sets --chip-bg to --color-accent-subtle, active to --color-accent-subtle-hover; pressed uses the inverse pair (--color-bg-inverse, --color-fg-inverse); disabled uses --color-bg-subtle and --color-fg-disabled.

Pressed chips are ink, not yellow, on purpose: the yellow is reserved for the one primary action and for “free” badges, and a row of yellow chips would compete with “Show the lineup”. In dark mode the inverse pair flips, so a pressed chip is a paper stamp on the charcoal page.

The tick is generated content (content: "✓"), which some screen readers read as “check mark” before the label, on top of “pressed”. It should carry empty alternative text: .chip[aria-pressed="true"]::before { content: "✓" / ""; }.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Filter chips are toggle buttons (APG Button pattern, aria-pressed) grouped by a <fieldset> and <legend>. Static tags are list items. A removable chip is text with a real <button> inside; the chip itself is not focusable.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the chips one by one (each is a tab stop), then on to the lineup.
Enter / SpaceToggles a filter chip; removes a removable chip.
After removingFocus moves to the next remove button, or to the previous one, or to the filters’ legend when none are left.

Focus

The shared 3px ring with a 3px offset around the chip, or around the × on a removable chip.

Labelling

The legend (“Filter by style”) names the group; each chip’s text is its name and aria-pressed its state. Counts include hidden words (“, 3 free”) so they make sense read aloud. Remove buttons say what they remove: “Remove filter: Gospel choir”. The lineup’s result count is announced in a polite status region after each change, not by the chip.

Disabled chips use --color-fg-disabled, which is exempt as an inactive control; they appear only for a second or two while results load.

Motion

Fill changes take --duration-fast; nothing moves or lifts. Instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Filter chips are 44px tall with 8px between them. Small chips (36px) are only used where rows are spaced 16px or more. The remove × is a 24px square inside a 44px chip.

Content

  • One to three words, sentence case in the source (CSS uppercases): “Gospel choir”, “Under $800”, “Spanish”.
  • Name the thing being filtered for, not the action: “Hymns”, not “Show hymns”.
  • Price and distance chips use Zamaro’s formats: “Under $800”, “Within 120 km”.
  • Counts are bare numbers: “Band 3”, with hidden words for screen readers.
  • The legend is short and says what the chips filter: “Filter by style”.
