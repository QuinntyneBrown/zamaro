Menu

A menu button opens a short list of actions or links, like the account menu behind Naomi’s “NF” avatar. Arrow keys move through it and Escape closes it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Menu ButtonClass .menu

Overview

Use a menu when a single button needs to offer three to eight related actions or destinations, and showing them all would crowd the view. Zamaro has three:

  • Account menu behind the “NF” avatar in the top bar: Naomi’s saved artists, booking requests, church profile, help and sign out.
  • Sort menu with checkable items, for compact toolbars such as the saved-artists list on a phone. The Discover lineup keeps its native select, because there sort is a form value next to other fields.
  • Artist actions behind a “more” button on a saved artist: share, message, remove from saved.

Do not use a menu for site navigation (use the top bar), for choosing a form value (use a select or radio group), or for content with inputs or paragraphs (that is a popover or dialog).

Anatomy

  1. TriggerAny button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded. Here the 40px avatar; elsewhere a .btn with a chevron or a ghost icon button.
  2. PanelRaised surface, 2px charcoal rule, hard 6px --shadow-2, 4px inner padding, at least 14rem wide. Opens 4px below the trigger, aligned to its right edge.
  3. Section labelMono overline in muted ink. It names the group for screen readers through aria-labelledby.
  4. Item44px tall: optional 20px icon, label in body small, optional trailing hint (a count or shortcut). Hover and focus fill it with the subtle yellow.
  5. Separator1px hairline with 4px above and below. Separates groups, never single items.

Variants

All menus share the panel and the item. They differ in what the items do.

Anchored account menu and drawer list

The product’s account menu and navigation drawer hold only links and one or two buttons, so they use a plain list rather than the ARIA menu pattern: .menu-list is a <ul role="list"> of .menu__item links that Tab reaches in order. The account menu is an anchored, non-dimming dialog.dialog--menu that hangs under the avatar; it opens with .menu__who (name, church, email) above a dashed rule. The navigation drawer (.dialog--drawer) uses the same list, marks the current page with aria-current="page", and ends with the theme toggle: a .menu__item button named “Dark theme” with aria-pressed, which shows a yellow inset bar and a filled icon when on.

Sizes

Menus have one size. Every item is 44px tall, so the list is easy to hit on a phone.

States

Item states are forced with data-state. In the product, hover comes from the pointer and focus from the arrow keys, and both look the same apart from the inset focus rule.

An open .btn trigger gets the subtle fill and drops its lift. The avatar has no open style of its own; the panel below it is the cue.

Both themes

  • Hover and focus fill the item with --color-accent-subtle. Focus adds a 2px inset rule in the focus colour, so keyboard users can tell focus from the mouse position.
  • Checked shows the tick. Nothing else changes: the tick is enough, and the trigger label repeats the choice.
  • Disabled greys the item and ignores clicks, but it stays in the arrow-key order.
  • Danger uses danger text and a pale red hover. It is for items that remove something from Naomi’s lists.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel is at least 14rem (224px) wide and grows to its longest item; the examples here set width: max-content inline until the stylesheet does. It opens under the trigger’s right edge, because triggers sit at the right of the top bar and of ticket stubs. At 360px it fits with the 16px page margin.
  • Item labels wrap rather than truncate. Keep them short so they don’t.
  • If a trigger sits on the left of a narrow screen, flip the panel to left: 0 so it does not open off-screen.
  • On phones, the top-bar hamburger opens the main navigation drawer; the avatar menu stays an anchored menu under the top bar (it is not a full-screen dialog). Below 992px the theme toggle is the last item of the drawer.
  • A menu with more than eight items is too long for a phone screen. Split it, or use a page.

Theming

The menu reads semantic tokens directly: --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-strong, --shadow-2, --color-accent-subtle for hover and focus, --color-focus-ring for the inset focus rule, --color-fg-muted for section labels and hints, --color-border-default for separators and --color-danger-fg / --color-danger-bg for danger items. It has no --menu-* component tokens yet.

In the dark theme the panel is a raised charcoal surface with a light rule, and its hard shadow turns yellow like every other lifted surface on the stage. The account menu opens from the stage top bar but is itself a paper (or charcoal) panel, not part of the stage.

Because the top bar re-colours its links (:is(.on-stage, .topbar) a:not(.btn):hover), a link item inside a menu that lives in the top bar would turn yellow with no fill on hover. Menus need to opt out of that rule: :is(.on-stage, .topbar) .menu a.menu__item:hover { background: var(--color-accent-subtle); color: var(--color-fg-default); }.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The APG Menu Button pattern. The trigger is a <button> with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls. The panel has role="menu" and aria-labelledby pointing at the trigger. Items are role="menuitem", menuitemradio or menuitemcheckbox with tabindex="-1". Groups are role="group" labelled by their section label; separators are role="separator".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / Space / ↓ on the triggerOpens the menu and focuses the first item.
↑ on the triggerOpens the menu and focuses the last item.
↓ / ↑Moves to the next or previous item, wrapping at the ends. Disabled items are included.
Home / EndMoves to the first or last item.
A letterMoves to the next item that starts with that letter (type-ahead).
EnterActivates the item and closes the menu. On a checkable item it sets the item and closes.
SpaceActivates an action item. On a checkable item it toggles the item and may leave the menu open.
EscCloses the menu and returns focus to the trigger.
TabCloses the menu and moves focus to the next element after the trigger.

Focus

Opening moves focus into the menu; closing returns it to the trigger, except when Tab closes it. Items are focused with element.focus() (a roving focus), so the browser scrolls them into view. The focused item gets the subtle fill plus a 2px inset rule in --color-focus-ring, drawn inside the item so the panel’s edge never clips it.

Labelling

The trigger’s name says whose menu it is: “Account menu for Naomi Fraser”, “More actions for Luz Viva”. Avoid a bare “More”. The menu takes its name from the trigger. Item names come from their text, so a trailing count is read too (“Saved artists 3”). Section labels are aria-hidden and used only as group names, so they are not read twice. Disabled items keep their reason in the text (“After booking”).

Disabled items use --color-fg-disabled, which WCAG exempts from contrast because the item is inactive. The reason text next to it should still be readable, so keep it short and rely on the screen-reader name as well.

Motion

The menu appears and disappears without animation. Item fills change instantly. Nothing here depends on motion.

Touch

Items are 44px tall and full width, so they meet the comfortable target. The avatar trigger is 40px; the 44px top bar row around it keeps the target comfortable. Tapping outside the menu closes it without acting.

Content

  • Items start with a verb for actions (“Share profile”, “Remove from saved”, “Sign out”) and a noun for destinations (“Saved artists”, “Booking requests”).
  • Sentence case, two or three words. No trailing ellipsis unless the item opens a dialog that asks for more.
  • Sort items describe the order, not the field: “Closest first”, “Price, low to high”.
  • The trigger says what is selected (“Sort: closest first”) or whose menu it is (aria-label on icon and avatar triggers).
  • Keyboard shortcuts, if ever added, need a modifier and go in aria-keyshortcuts; single letters are taken by type-ahead. Today .menu__shortcut holds counts and short hints.
