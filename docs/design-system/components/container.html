Container & stack

The layout primitives every page is built from. A container sets the page width and margins, stacks and clusters set the space between things, and the grid lines them up in 4, 8 or 12 columns.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableLayout only, no roleClasses .container .stack .cluster .grid .section

Overview

Use these classes for layout instead of writing margins and widths on components. Components then stay free of outer spacing and can be dropped into any page.

  • .container centres content, caps it at 1216px and adds the page margin (20, 40 or 64px by breakpoint).
  • .stack puts children in a column with one gap between them. .cluster puts them in a wrapping row, such as a row of buttons or badges.
  • .grid is the column grid; .grid--cards fills a row with as many 288px-minimum cards as fit.
  • .section is a band of the page with generous top and bottom padding and a hairline between neighbours. .section__head holds its kicker, heading and an optional aside.
  • .profile-layout is the artist page: content on the left, the booking ticket in a 352px column on the right that stays in view.

Spacing between and around things comes from the spacing scale; columns, gutters and margins from the layout foundation.

Anatomy

  1. MarginThe container’s side padding, --layout-margin: 20px on phones, 40px from 768px, 64px from 992px. The dashed outline is the container.
  2. Section head.section__head: kicker and heading on the left, an aside (sort, a note) on the right; wraps on phones.
  3. Stack gapThe space between the head and the grid: .stack is 16px, .stack--lg 40px.
  4. Columns and gutters--layout-columns (4, 8, 12) separated by --layout-gutter (16, 24, 32px). Resize the window: the numbered cells re-flow.

Detail layout

.detail-layout is the two-column page for one record (a booking, a request, an application): the main column of panels and a 22rem (--layout-sidebar-width) <aside> that sticks under the top bar from 992px. Below 992px it is one column with the aside last. .detail-layout--aside-first moves the aside straight after the page head below 992px, for pages whose one primary action lives in the aside (the booker’s payment panel), so it is never below a long thread of messages.

Stacks and clusters come in sizes; pick by how related the children are. Closely related things sit closer.

.stack--sm --space-2 · .stack --space-4 · .stack--lg --space-10. Clusters: --space-2 and .cluster--lg --space-4.

Sections have two sizes: .section (64px top and bottom, 80px from 992px) and .section--tight (40px) for short bands such as the call-to-action band.

Responsive behaviour

  • Mobile first: base styles are for 360px; tokens.css raises --layout-columns, --layout-gutter and --layout-margin at 48rem and 62rem. Components read the tokens rather than writing their own media queries.
  • Containers never cause sideways scrolling: grid tracks are minmax(0, 1fr), and card minimums are min(100%, 18rem) so a card is never wider than a phone.
  • Clusters wrap; stacks never do. A cluster of buttons becomes two rows on a phone rather than shrinking buttons.
  • On the profile, the ticket follows the reviews below 992px; “Book for Sat 14 Nov” in the poster jumps to it.
  • .page-head wraps: the action or stamp drops under the title on narrow screens; it never shrinks the h1.
  • .detail-layout: one column below 992px (aside last, or first with --aside-first); from 992px (--layout-breakpoint-lg) main column plus a sticky 22rem aside, 64px apart.
  • .auth-layout fills 100dvh minus the top bar and centres the card at every width; the card is at most --layout-auth-width (28rem).

Theming

No colours. The primitives read layout and spacing tokens only: --layout-container-max, --layout-margin, --layout-columns, --layout-gutter, --layout-sidebar-width, --layout-topbar-height (for the sticky ticket’s offset) and --space-*. The only colour is the hairline between sections, --color-border-default.

To change the grid inside one region, override the tokens on that element, as the “Both themes” specimens above do: style="--layout-columns: 4". Do not edit the classes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

None: these are presentational <div> wrappers. Meaning comes from the elements you put them on: <section aria-labelledby> for page sections, <aside> for the booking ticket, <ul role="list"> for a grid of tickets. No APG pattern applies.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFollows DOM order. Layout must not reorder content visually (no order, no grid placement that moves things out of source order), so the tab order matches what people see.

Focus

The sticky ticket in .profile-layout sits below the top bar (top: calc(--layout-topbar-height + --space-6)), and the page sets scroll-padding-top, so focused fields are never hidden behind either (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

Each .section is a <section> labelled by its heading (aria-labelledby), which makes it a region landmark. Kickers (“About”, “Live”) are plain paragraphs before the heading; they are read first, so keep them short.

Motion

None. Layout changes at breakpoints are instant.

Touch

Clusters keep at least 8px between targets; button rows use .cluster--lg (16px). At 200% and 400% zoom the grid drops to 4 columns, so nothing needs two-dimensional scrolling (WCAG 1.4.10).

Content

  • Section kickers are short labels in the stub style: “About”, “Live”, “On tour”, “Word of mouth”, or the search context “Sat 14 Nov · 7 free · within 120 km”.
  • Section headings say something specific: “Raised in the choir loft”, “Watch her lead”, not “Biography”, “Videos”.
  • The aside in .section__head is one short sentence or one control: “Cancel free up to 14 days before your event.” or the sort field.
  • Prose inside a section stays under --layout-measure (58 characters) per line; paragraphs do this by default.
