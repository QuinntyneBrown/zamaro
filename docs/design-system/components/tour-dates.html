Tour dates

Tour dates show when an artist is free, the way a gig poster lists its dates. Naomi’s date is stamped in yellow; any other free date can be picked instead.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <ul> + aria-current="date"Class .tour-dates

Overview

Use tour dates on an artist’s profile to show the next few weeks of availability around the date the booker searched for. Each row is one day: the date, a short note, and whether the artist is Free or Booked. Free days have a small “Pick” button that changes the booking to that day.

  • It is a short list (six to eight days), not a calendar. For choosing any date, use the date picker in the booking stub.
  • The booker’s own date is marked with aria-current="date" and a yellow day stamp, so Naomi finds it first.
  • Booked rows say what the artist is doing where the artist allows it (“Sunday service, Oakville”), so the gap looks real.

Anatomy

  1. Day stamp.tour-dates__day: weekday in a mono overline above the date in the 24px figure face. Yellow with ink text on the current date.
  2. Note.tour-dates__note: muted 14px. “Your date”, “Open all day” or what the artist is doing.
  3. StatusA Free or Booked badge. On free rows it moves beside the note to make room for Pick.
  4. Pick (free dates only)A small secondary button, named “Pick {date}”.
  5. RowThree-column grid (104px · flexible · auto), 16px block padding, hairline below.

Where nothing can be booked from the list (an artist viewing their own dates, or a printed confirmation), drop the Pick buttons and keep every badge in the trailing column.

Sizes

Tour dates have one size. The Pick button is always small; the row gives it a 44px-plus target.

States

Rows themselves are not interactive. The Pick button has the button states; the day stamp and the badge change with the data. Hover and focus are forced with data-state.

  • Picking: pressing Pick sets aria-busy="true" on it while the stub checks the date. When done, that row becomes aria-current="date" (yellow stamp, note “Your date”, no Pick), the old current row gets a Pick button back, and the page’s status region says “Date changed to Fri 20 Nov”.

  • Loading: four rows of skeletons (.skeleton--figure for the date, .skeleton--text for the note).
  • Empty: “Abigail hasn’t shared dates past Sat 28 Nov. Ask about yours in your message.” in a quiet empty state.
  • Error: a compact danger alert: “We couldn’t load Abigail’s dates. Your booking form still works.”

Responsive behaviour

  • The three-column row works from 320px up: the day column is fixed at 6.5rem, the trail is as wide as its badge or button, and the middle column takes the rest.
  • On free rows the badge sits beside the note in a .cluster, which wraps the note under the badge on phones instead of squeezing the date.
  • Pick never wraps or truncates. Notes wrap freely; keep them under about 35 characters so a row stays two lines at 360px.
  • On desktop the list sits in the profile’s main column beside the sticky booking stub, so picking a date updates the stub without scrolling.

Theming

Tour dates have no component tokens. The date is --color-fg-default, the weekday and note are --color-fg-muted, rules are --color-border-default. The current day stamp fills with --color-accent and switches both lines to --color-fg-on-accent. Badges and the Pick button bring their own component tokens (--badge-*, --btn-*).

In the dark theme the yellow stamp stays yellow with ink text, the brightest mark in the list, and the Pick button turns into a charcoal surface with a light outline.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <ul role="list"> of days, labelled by its section heading. The booker’s date carries aria-current="date", which screen readers announce as “current date”. Pick is an ordinary <button> (APG Button pattern). It is not a grid or a calendar: there is no arrow-key navigation.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between Pick buttons, top to bottom. Booked and current rows have no stop.
Enter / SpacePicks that date for the booking.

Focus

The shared ring on the Pick button. After a pick the button disappears from that row, so focus moves to the booking stub’s date field, which now shows the new date and its “Abigail is free Fri 20 Nov” message. Focus never drops to the top of the page.

Labelling

Each Pick button has aria-label="Pick Fri 20 Nov": the visible word first, then the date, so eight buttons don’t all read “Pick” (WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name). Rows read as weekday, date, note and status: “Sat 14 Nov Your date Free, current date”. Badge words carry the status; the yellow stamp and strike-through are extras.

Motion

The Pick button lifts on hover like every secondary button and shows a spinner while busy. Moving the current date is instant; rows never slide. Reduced motion zeroes the lift.

Touch

Pick is 36px tall inside a row with 16px padding above and below, so its effective target is over 44px. Pick buttons in neighbouring rows are at least 32px apart.

Content

  • Weekday as three letters above the date: “Sat” over “14 Nov”. No year: the list covers a few weeks.
  • The booker’s date says “Your date”, followed by what is happening if it is booked (“Your date · Christmas Eve, Scarborough”).
  • Free notes are short facts: “Open all day”, “Evenings only”.
  • Booked notes name the gathering and town, only with the artist’s permission; otherwise just “Booked”.
  • The button is always “Pick”, with the date in its accessible name.
  • Show the six to eight days nearest the booker’s date, in date order.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The mocks gave Pick an aria-label with the date; that pattern is kept and is now the rule for every row button.
  • Skeleton heights were 0.9rem and 1.8rem with inline widths. Loading rows now use .skeleton--figure and .skeleton--text at 1rem steps.
  • Free badges read the primitive --palette-ink for their label and outline. They now use --color-fg-on-accent and --color-border-on-accent.
  • Inline display-font shorthands for dates became the --text-figure role token.
