Booking list

The tour book: one framed row per booking or request, with the date set big on the left, who and what in the middle, the money and the status stamp on the right, and the next action beside the stamp.

Overview

A booking list shows many bookings of one person at once, ordered by date, so they can see what is coming and act on the one that needs them. Naomi’s Your bookings (L2-033) and Abigail’s Requests inbox (L2-034) both use it, as do “Needs your reply” on the artist dashboard and the Upcoming and Paid out lists on Earnings. One .booking-list__row as used on pages/requests is the “requests inbox row” composite, which has its own perf-test scenario (AGENTS.md, component perf tests); the same row serves the booker’s bookings list.

  • Use it for a person’s own bookings or requests, where each row leads to the booking page and has at most one next action.
  • Use a ticket card for artists in search results; use a table when people compare many columns or select rows (the admin queue); use tour dates for a short list of an artist’s dates on a profile.
  • The whole row is one link target (the name link stretches over the row); the status stamp and the action button sit above it and stay separately clickable.

Anatomy

  1. Date.booking-list__date: day of the week as a mono overline over the date in the display face, 32px. Always the event date.
  2. Who.booking-list__who: a heading holding the link to the booking; the link’s ::after covers the whole row.
  3. Meta.booking-list__meta: booking number, kind of gathering, start time, place and distance, amount; muted, 14px.
  4. Amount.booking-list__amount: the figure again, big, from MD (768px). Hidden from assistive technology because the meta already says it.
  5. Status.booking-list__status: the status stamp and the one next action (or the time left to reply).

Requests (artist)

Requested bookings ordered by reply deadline. The meta carries three lines: the event, the quoted price and the payout after the fee, and the start of the church’s message. The status line gives the time left instead of a button (L2-034).

Compact (dashboard, earnings)

The same rows under a section heading. “Needs your reply” on the dashboard shows the requests waiting on the artist, with the message preview and the reply deadline as in the inbox. “Upcoming” and “Paid out” on Earnings replace the preview with the money line (total, Zamaro fee, payout) and show a payment badge (“Deposit held”) in place of the stamp.

Sizes

The booking list has one size. Rows fill their container; the date column is 5.5rem on phones and 6.5rem from MD.

Loading
  • Hover and focus within lift the row by 4px with the first hard shadow, like a ticket; the focus ring is on the name link or the button that has focus.
  • Loading (.booking-list--loading) keeps the row frame and puts skeletons in each slot so the swap causes no layout shift (L2-105). Rows are aria-hidden; the page’s aria-busy="true" region and a visually hidden status line (“Loading your bookings”) speak for them. Loading rows don’t lift.
  • Empty and no results are not states of the list: the list is left out and an empty state takes its place (bookings/empty, requests/no-results).
  • Error replaces the list with an alert and Try again (bookings/error).

Responsive behaviour

  • Below MD (768px): two columns. The date spans the first two lines on the left; who and meta stack on the right; the status line (stamp left, action right) runs full width underneath. The amount is hidden because the meta already says it.
  • From MD (768px): four columns, date · who and meta · amount · status, vertically centred; the date column widens to 6.5rem and the status aligns right.
  • Long church names wrap inside their column; the date never wraps. Nothing scrolls sideways at 320px.

Theming

The booking list has no component tokens. Rows are --color-bg-surface framed in --color-border-strong at --border-width-thick; the day, meta and amount labels read --color-fg-muted. The hover lift reuses --transform-lift and --shadow-1, which is ink on newsprint and yellow on the stage. In the dark theme rows become charcoal paper on the charcoal canvas, framed in paper.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native list (<ol> when ordered by date or deadline, <ul> otherwise) with role="list" so Safari keeps list semantics after list-style: none, and an aria-label (“Upcoming bookings”, “Past bookings”). Each row starts with a heading at the right level for the page, so people can jump row to row with heading keys.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the row’s name link, then to its action button, then to the next row.
EnterOn the name link, opens the booking or request; on the button, does the action.

Focus

The focus ring is drawn on the focused link or button (two-tone ring, 3px offset). The row also lifts on :focus-within so the active row is clear at a glance. The stretched link’s ::after covers the row, and the stamp and buttons sit above it at --z-raised, so pointer users can click anywhere and the button still works.

Labelling

The name link’s text is the artist or church name; the row is read in source order: date, name, meta, stamp, action. Write the amount in the meta (“Paid $237.50”) and mark the big .booking-list__amount aria-hidden="true" so it is not read twice. Buttons repeat the object only when needed: “Pay $225 deposit” is clear on its own; several “Message” buttons in a row are disambiguated by the heading before them.

Motion

The hover lift is a 160ms transform; under prefers-reduced-motion durations drop to zero and the row changes state without moving.

Touch

The whole row is a tap target for the booking; the small action button grows to 44px on touch devices, and sits at least 12px from the stamp.

Content

  • Date: short day and date in the L2-110 pattern (“Sat” over “14 Nov”); add the year only outside the current year.
  • Who: the other party, as they name themselves (“Abigail Mensah”, “Harvest Point Church”).
  • Meta, separated by middle dots: booking number · kind of gathering · start time (“7:00 p.m.”) · place, distance (“Brampton, 44 km”) · amount (“Paid $237.50”). For requests: the event line, then “$800 quoted · you get $736”, then the first words of the message in <q>.
  • Action: one verb phrase with the amount when money moves (“Pay $225 deposit”). Time left to reply leads with the number: “1 day left · reply by Sat 10 Oct, 3:15 p.m.”.
