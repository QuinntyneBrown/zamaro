Radio group

A radio group asks for exactly one answer and shows every option: what sound the church has, or what kind of gathering it is. Pick one and the others let go.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <input type="radio">Classes .radio .choice

Overview

Use a radio group when Naomi must pick exactly one of two to six options and seeing them side by side helps her choose. With more options, or options that need no comparison, use a select. For yes/no settings use a single checkbox.

  • Vertical (.choice-group): the default. Options can carry a one-line description.
  • Inline (.choice-group--inline): two to four one- or two-word options.

Radios are round. Everything else in Zamaro is square, but a round dot is how people everywhere recognise “pick one”, and a square radio reads as a checkbox. This is a deliberate exception, shared only with the save toggle and the avatar.

Anatomy

  1. LegendThe question, as a stamped overline. It names the group and is read before each option.
  2. Dot24px circle, 2px charcoal rule. Selected: yellow fill, ink rule, 10px ink centre.
  3. LabelBody text; the whole row is the target.
  4. Description (optional)Body small, muted. One sentence.

Variants

Vertical, with descriptions

Choice row (forms and dialogs)

The dialogs in the mocks ask single-choice questions with the light .choice row (see Checkbox): a native radio drawn as a round 20px dot with appearance: none, then .choice__text and an optional <small> detail. Use it for reasons and short answers in dialogs and forms (decline a request, the application). The report-review and resolve-hold dialogs use the full .radio row with a drawn dot instead; both are documented here, and both are native radios in a <fieldset>.

Why are you declining? (optional)
From dialogs/decline-request: checked, unchecked with a detail line, and disabled.
HTML
<fieldset class="choice-group">
  <legend>Why are you declining? <span class="field__optional">(optional)</span></legend>
  <label class="choice"><input type="radio" name="reason" value="free"><span class="choice__text">I’m not free that day</span></label>
  <label class="choice"><input type="radio" name="reason" value="far"><span class="choice__text">It’s too far to travel<small>Burlington is 44 km from Brampton</small></span></label>
</fieldset>

Star rating (write a review)

The only star input in the product is a radio group: five .choice.choice--stub cards in a .cluster, one per score, each with the stars drawn as aria-hidden text, a visually hidden “{n} out of 5 stars” and a word under it. It gives every score a name, a large target and arrow-key support, which clickable star widgets usually lack (see Rating).

Your rating (required)
From dialogs/write-review (L2-059): Unforgettable, Great, Good, Fair, Poor. The checked card takes the yellow tint of .choice--stub; the ring follows focus with the arrow keys. In edit mode the church’s score is checked.
HTML
<fieldset class="fieldset">
  <legend>Your rating <span class="field__required">(required)</span></legend>
  <div class="cluster">
    <label class="choice choice--stub"><input type="radio" name="stars" value="5">
      <span class="choice__text"><span aria-hidden="true">★★★★★</span><span class="visually-hidden">5 out of 5 stars</span><small>Unforgettable</small></span></label>
    …4 Great, 3 Good, 2 Fair, 1 Poor
  </div>
</fieldset>

Sizes

Radios have one size (a 24px dot).

Responsive behaviour

  • Vertical groups are the same at every width. Inline groups wrap; if they wrap at 360px, make them vertical.

Theming

The dot shares the checkbox’s component tokens: --control-bg, --control-border and --control-fg. Selected sets --control-bg to --color-accent and the border to --color-border-on-accent; the centre is currentColor of the dot, ink on yellow in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input type="radio"> with a shared name, in a <fieldset> with a <legend>. This gives the APG Radio Group pattern without ARIA.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves into the group (to the selected radio, or the first if none) and out of it. The group is one tab stop.
↓ / →Selects the next radio, wrapping to the first.
↑ / ←Selects the previous radio, wrapping to the last.
SpaceSelects the focused radio when none is selected yet.

Focus

The ring is drawn on the dot (input:focus-visible + .radio__dot). Arrow keys move focus and selection together, so the ring always sits on the selected option.

Labelling

The legend names the group; each label names its option. Descriptions are inside the label and part of the name. A group-level error is linked from the fieldset with aria-describedby, and every radio gets aria-invalid. Do not put required on more than the first radio; the browser treats the group as one.

Contrast

Dot rule on the surface
Selected centre on yellow
Dark: dot rule on the surface

Motion

The dot fills over --duration-fast. Under reduced motion it is instant.

Touch

Each row is a full-width label, at least 24px tall and 12px from the next. Inline options are 24px apart.

Content

  • The legend asks the question: “Indoors or outdoors?”, “Kind of gathering”.
  • Options are parallel and mutually exclusive. If two could both be true, it is a checkbox group.
  • Pre-select the most common answer when it is safe (Sunday service). Leave the group empty when a guess could cost the church money.
