Radio group

A radio group asks for exactly one answer and shows every option: what sound the church has, or what kind of gathering it is. Pick one and the others let go.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <input type="radio">Classes .radio .choice-card

Overview

Use a radio group when Naomi must pick exactly one of two to six options and seeing them side by side helps her choose. With more options, or options that need no comparison, use a select. For yes/no settings use a switch or a single checkbox.

  • Vertical (.choice-group): the default. Options can carry a one-line description.
  • Inline (.choice-group--inline): two to four one- or two-word options.
  • Choice cards (.choice-card): bigger targets with a title and a detail line, for a choice that shapes the rest of the form, like “Kind of gathering” at the start of a request.

Radios are round. Everything else in Zamaro is square, but a round dot is how people everywhere recognise “pick one”, and a square radio reads as a checkbox. This is a deliberate exception, shared only with the save toggle, the switch track and the avatar.

Anatomy

  1. LegendThe question, as a stamped overline. It names the group and is read before each option.
  2. Dot24px circle, 2px charcoal rule. Selected: yellow fill, ink rule, 10px ink centre.
  3. LabelBody text; the whole row is the target.
  4. Description (optional)Body small, muted. One sentence.

Sizes

Radios have one size (a 24px dot). Choice cards size to their grid column.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state on the label. Forced focus on a .radio row sets outline: none inline, so only the dot shows the ring, as it does in the product.

Choose indoors or outdoors so the artist can plan for sound.

Every radio in the group gets aria-invalid; the fieldset is described by the error.

Both themes

Known issue in dark mode: the selected choice card’s frame uses --color-border-on-accent (ink), which nearly disappears on the dark surface (about 1.2:1), and its amber fill is also close to the surface. Until it is fixed, the selected card in dark mode relies on its fill alone. See Theming for the proposed token.

Responsive behaviour

  • Vertical groups are the same at every width. Inline groups wrap; if they wrap at 360px, make them vertical.
  • Choice cards sit in .grid--cards: one column on phones, as many 18rem columns as fit above that.
  • Card titles wrap; keep them to two or three words so a row of cards has even heights.

Theming

The dot shares the checkbox’s component tokens: --control-bg, --control-border and --control-fg. Selected sets --control-bg to --color-accent and the border to --color-border-on-accent; the centre is currentColor of the dot, ink on yellow in both themes.

Choice cards have one component token, --choice-border (--color-border-strong), set to --color-border-on-accent when selected, plus a --color-accent-subtle fill. In dark mode both are too close to the charcoal surface. Proposed fix: a theme-aware --color-border-selected token (ink --palette-ink-750 on paper, yellow --palette-signal-500 on the stage) used for --choice-border and the inset ring of selected cards.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input type="radio"> with a shared name, in a <fieldset> with a <legend>. This gives the APG Radio Group pattern without ARIA. Choice cards are the same radios with a larger label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves into the group (to the selected radio, or the first if none) and out of it. The group is one tab stop.
↓ / →Selects the next radio, wrapping to the first.
↑ / ←Selects the previous radio, wrapping to the last.
SpaceSelects the focused radio when none is selected yet.

Focus

The ring is drawn on the dot (input:focus-visible + .radio__dot), and around the whole card for choice cards (.choice-card:has(> input:focus-visible)). Arrow keys move focus and selection together, so the ring always sits on the selected option.

Labelling

The legend names the group; each label names its option. Descriptions are inside the label and part of the name. A group-level error is linked from the fieldset with aria-describedby, and every radio gets aria-invalid. Do not put required on more than the first radio; the browser treats the group as one.

Motion

The dot fills over --duration-fast. Cards lift on hover over --duration-fast; under reduced motion both are instant.

Touch

Each row is a full-width label, at least 24px tall and 12px from the next. The whole choice card is the target. Inline options are 24px apart.

Content

  • The legend asks the question: “Indoors or outdoors?”, “Kind of gathering”.
  • Options are parallel and mutually exclusive. If two could both be true, it is a checkbox group.
  • Pre-select the most common answer when it is safe (Sunday service). Leave the group empty when a guess could cost the church money.
  • Card detail lines use a middle dot: “Three or four songs · about 25 min”.
