Save toggle

The round heart on a ticket saves an artist to Naomi’s shortlist. One press saves, another removes, and the yellow fill shows which artists are saved.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableToggle aria-pressedClass .save

Overview

Use the save toggle on tickets and on the headliner, where there is room for a 44px circle but not for a label. It adds the artist to the booker’s saved list and bumps the count on the top bar’s “Saved” button.

  • Off: a paper circle with an outlined heart. Pressing it saves the artist.
  • On: a yellow circle with a filled heart. Pressing it removes the artist from saved.

On the artist profile there is room for words, so the same action is a labelled toggle button, “Save” / “Saved”: see Button. Do not use the save toggle for anything but saving artists; the heart means one thing across Zamaro.

Saving is instant and reversible, so it never asks for confirmation. A polite toast confirms it (“Saved Luz Viva”, with Undo), and the top-bar count updates.

Anatomy

  1. Circle44px (--target-comfortable), fully round, 2px rule. The one round control in a square system, so a save toggle is recognisable at a glance on every ticket.
  2. Heart icon20px stroke heart, aria-hidden. Outlined when off, filled when on.
  3. Pressed fillButter yellow with an ink rule and an ink heart (--color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent). The same pressed look as the “Saved” button.

Variants

There is one control with two values. What changes is where it sits.

Off and on

The label says what pressing will do and names the artist: “Save Elijah Park to your saved artists”, “Remove Abigail Mensah from your saved artists”.

Try it

Press the heart to save Elijah Park.

The toggle flips at once, pulses while the request is out (aria-busy), then a status message confirms it. The product uses a toast for that message.

Sizes

The save toggle has one size: a 44px circle, the comfortable touch target.

States

Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state. Busy and disabled use the real attributes.

ValueDefaultHoverFocusActiveBusyDisabled
Off
On

Both themes

  • Hover grows the circle by 8% on the spring easing. It does not lift or cast a shadow, so it does not compete with the ticket’s own lift.
  • Active squeezes it to 94%, like pressing a rubber stamp.
  • Busy sets aria-busy="true" while the save is sent. The heart pulses and clicks are ignored. The value has already flipped (optimistic update); if the request fails, flip it back and show a danger toast: “Couldn’t save Luz Viva. Try again.”
  • Disabled is rare: only while the lineup itself is loading. A signed-out visitor gets an enabled toggle that asks them to sign in.

Responsive behaviour

  • The toggle is the same 44px at every width. It never shrinks inside a cramped stub.
  • In a ticket it moves with the stub: bottom-right on phones, bottom of the right-hand stub column from 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm).
  • It keeps at least 8px from the price and from the ticket edge, so a thumb aimed at the heart does not open the profile.
  • Touch devices get no hover growth; the pressed fill is the only feedback they need.

Theming

The toggle has three component tokens: --save-bg (default --color-bg-surface), --save-fg (--color-fg-default) and --save-border (--color-border-strong). Pressed sets them to --color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent and --color-border-on-accent; disabled to --color-bg-subtle and --color-fg-disabled. A surface that needs a different look overrides the three tokens, not the rules.

.save { --save-bg: var(--color-bg-surface); --save-fg: var(--color-fg-default); --save-border: var(--color-border-strong); }
.save[aria-pressed="true"] { --save-bg: var(--color-accent); --save-fg: var(--color-fg-on-accent); --save-border: var(--color-border-on-accent); }

In dark mode the off toggle is a charcoal circle with a light rule. The pressed toggle keeps the butter yellow and the ink heart in both themes, so a saved artist looks the same on paper and on the stage.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button type="button"> with aria-pressed, the toggle form of the APG Button pattern. It is never a checkbox: saving is an action that takes effect at once, not a form value.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the toggle. On a ticket it comes after the artist’s name link.
Enter / SpaceSaves or removes the artist. Focus stays on the toggle.

Focus

The shared 3px ring with a 3px offset, ink on paper and yellow in dark mode. It follows the square outline box around the circle. When the toggle is inside a ticket, the ticket also lifts (:focus-within), which helps keyboard users see which artist they are on.

Labelling

The icon is aria-hidden, so the aria-label is the only name, and it must name the artist. The label changes with the value: “Save Elijah Park to your saved artists” when off, “Remove Abigail Mensah from your saved artists” when on. With aria-pressed a screen reader adds the state: “Remove Abigail Mensah from your saved artists, toggle button, pressed”. The APG prefers a label that stays the same when pressed; if usability testing shows the changing label confuses people, keep “Save Abigail Mensah” and let “pressed” carry the state. The result is announced politely by the toast (“Saved Luz Viva”), and the top-bar count’s label updates (“4 saved artists”).

The state never relies on colour alone: the heart is outlined when off and filled when on.

Motion

The grow and squeeze take --duration-fast on --ease-spring; the busy pulse loops on --duration-loop. Under prefers-reduced-motion the scale changes are instant. The pulse keeps running because it reports status, but it only fades opacity and never moves.

Touch

44 × 44px, the comfortable target. In a ticket stub it keeps 8px clear of the price and the card edge. Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Off: “Save {artist} to your saved artists”. On: “Remove {artist} from your saved artists”. Use the artist’s full display name, with “and” for “&” (“Daniel and Ruth Okonkwo”).
  • The confirmation toast is short and past tense: “Saved Luz Viva”, “Removed Luz Viva from saved”, with an Undo action.
  • Never add a visible label next to the circle. If the space allows a label, use the “Save” / “Saved” button instead.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The mock note promised a “Saved Luz Viva” toast, but no toast existed and the toast region sat high to clear the mock bar. There is now a toast component, and the region sits at --space-6.
  • The profile’s aria-pressed="true" “Saved” button had no pressed style. It now matches the circle: yellow fill, filled heart.
  • Busy and disabled states did not exist in the mocks. They were added so a double tap cannot save and unsave, and so the toggle is inert while the lineup loads.
