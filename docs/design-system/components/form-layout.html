Form layout

Forms on Zamaro are short tickets: one column of fields, a perforated line, then one yellow button. This page covers the arrangement, the error summary and what happens while a request is sending.

Overview

Use these classes to arrange form fields into a form. They handle spacing, columns, the actions bar and the error summary; the fields handle labels and messages.

  • Single column (.form): the default. Fields 20px apart, the form capped at 36rem so lines stay short.
  • Two-column row (.form__row): two short, related fields side by side from 768px, such as first and last day.
  • Inline (.form__inline): fields and a button on one line that wraps, for a quick search.
  • Actions bar (.form__actions): the submit and secondary buttons below a dashed perforation, like a ticket stub.
  • Error summary (.error-summary): after a failed submit, a list of what to fix with links to each field.
  • Submitting (.form[aria-busy="true"]): fields fade and stop taking input; the submit shows a spinner.

Zamaro’s two real forms are the booking bar on the Discover poster and the booking stub on an artist’s profile. Both are shown below. The full pattern, including validation timing, is in Forms.

Anatomy

  1. Error summaryAppears at the top after a failed submit and takes focus. Danger tint, 2px red rule, a title that counts the problems, one link per field.
  2. FieldsA column with 20px (--space-5) between fields.
  3. PerforationA 2px dashed charcoal rule above the actions, with 16px above the buttons. It separates “what you told us” from “send it”.
  4. ActionsThe primary button first, then any secondary. Right-aligned by default; .form__actions--start aligns them left.

  • Ready: fields prefilled with what Zamaro knows; errors appear only after leaving a field or submitting.
  • Invalid: the summary at the top takes focus and lists each problem as a link; each field shows its own error.
  • Submitting: aria-busy on the form and the button, a polite “Sending your request to Abigail”, no double submit.
  • Sent: the form is replaced by a confirmation with the booking code, and focus moves to it. Values become read-only if shown again.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column below 768px (--layout-breakpoint-md). .form__row becomes two equal columns from 768px; the DOM order is the reading order at every width.
  • The booking bar pairs its fields from 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm); its title and submit always span the full width.
  • The booking stub becomes a sticky sidebar from 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg), below the top bar. Its submit is always full width (.btn--block).
  • .form__actions wraps; on phones the buttons stack in DOM order, primary first.
  • .form__inline fields grow from 12rem; the button drops under the field when space runs out.

Theming

Form layout has no component tokens of its own. It reads spacing tokens (--space-3, --space-4, --space-5), the perforation (--border-width-thick dashed in --color-border-strong) and the danger set for the summary (--color-danger-bg, --color-danger-border, --color-danger-fg). Fields inside carry the field tokens (--field-bg, --field-fg, --field-border).

The booking bar is a paper island: data-theme="light" re-applies the light semantic tokens inside it, so it stays newsprint on the dark stage in both themes. The stub has no such attribute and turns charcoal in dark mode, like every other card. Its shadow is --shadow-3, which turns yellow in dark mode.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <form> named by its heading (aria-labelledby), with novalidate so Zamaro’s own messages replace the browser’s bubbles. Search forms add role="search". Follow the W3C WAI forms tutorial on user notifications for the summary and success messages.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields and buttons in reading order, top to bottom, left to right in rows.
Enter in a text fieldSubmits the form (not in a textarea).
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field and scrolls it below the sticky top bar.

Focus

After a failed submit, focus moves to the error summary (tabindex="-1"). After success, focus moves to the confirmation heading. While submitting, focus stays on the busy button. scroll-padding-top keeps focused fields clear of the sticky top bar (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

  • The summary is labelled by its title and announced by taking focus; it has no role="alert", which would read it twice. The title counts the problems; each link text names the field and the fix, and its href is the field’s id.
  • Groups of checkboxes or radios are fieldsets with legends; rows of unrelated fields are not.
  • Submitting is announced in a polite status region; the form and button carry aria-busy="true".
  • Never disable the submit to signal “not ready”: disabled buttons are skipped by Tab and explain nothing.

Busy fields fade to 60% opacity. They are not interactive while faded, and they return to full contrast as soon as the request finishes or fails.

Motion

The summary appears without animation; scrolling to it is smooth unless the user prefers reduced motion. The busy spinner keeps turning under reduced motion because it reports status.

Touch

20px between fields, 12px between action buttons, 44px fields and a 56px block submit in the stub, all reachable with a thumb on a 360px phone.

Content

  • The form heading names the task and the person: “Book Abigail”, “Your event”.
  • The submit names the action and the key detail: “Request to book · Sat 14 Nov”, “Show the lineup”.
  • Put reassurance next to the submit, not in a tooltip: “Nothing is charged until Abigail accepts. Cancel free up to 14 days before.”
  • The summary title says how many and why: “Fix 2 things to send your request”. Links read as instructions: “Pick a date Abigail is free”.
  • Busy labels are present tense: “Sending request…”, “Checking calendars…”.
  • Success gives the booking code and the next step: “Request sent · No. ZAM-0114. We’ll email you when Abigail replies.”
