Settings navigation

In-page links that jump between the sections of one long settings form: Naomi’s account and Abigail’s profile editor.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<nav> + linksClass .settings-nav

Overview

Use the settings nav beside a settings form long enough to scroll through several sections. It jumps within one page, not between pages, so the current item takes aria-current="true", not "page".

Moving between pages uses the top bar and its menu. Two or three views of one list, such as “Requests · Upcoming · Past”, use tabs.

Anatomy

Profile Church Email Password Signed-in devices Delete account
  1. Landmark: a <nav> named “Settings sections”.
  2. Links: one per form section, 44px tall, in uppercase label type.
  3. Current marker: a 4px yellow bar on the left in the column, a yellow underline in the row.

Variants

One component, laid out two ways by width. It sits in .settings-layout (see Form layout).

Sizes

One size: 44px links (--target-comfortable) and a 14rem column from 992px.

States

  • Default: muted label text.
  • Hover: a pale yellow fill and full-strength text.
  • Focus: the shared 3px two-tone ring.
  • Current (aria-current="true"): full-strength text and the yellow bar or underline.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 992px: a horizontally scrolling row above the form, with no visible scrollbar and 44px targets.
  • 992px and wider (--layout-breakpoint-lg): a sticky 14rem column under the top bar, beside the form.

Theming

The nav has no fill of its own and follows the theme: --color-fg-muted for links, --color-fg-default for the current and hovered link, --color-accent-subtle for hover and --color-accent for the current marker.

Accessibility

A <nav> landmark with a unique aria-label, so it is told apart from the “Primary” nav. The current section has aria-current="true", and the marker is never the only cue: the text also turns full strength.

Content

  • Each label is the heading of the section it jumps to, word for word.
  • Keep the form’s order, with destructive sections (“Delete account”) last.

Do and don't

  • Do use it for the sections of one page, with aria-current="true".
  • Don't use it to move between pages; that is the top bar’s job.
