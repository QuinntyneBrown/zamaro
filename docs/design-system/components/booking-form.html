Booking ticket

Zamaro’s two booking forms are printed like admission tickets: a paper bar in the Discover poster that asks for the date and place, and a stub on each profile that sends the request.

Overview

Both forms are tickets: a paper card with a 2px charcoal frame, a dashed perforation under the title, and a hard offset shadow. They are built from the standard form field, text field, select, textarea and button.

  • Booking bar (.booking-bar) lives in the Discover poster. It asks four things (date, kind of gathering, place, how far) and submits “Show the lineup”. It always has data-theme="light", so it stays paper on the charcoal stage, even in the dark theme.
  • Booking stub (.stub) lives in the right column of an artist profile. It shows the price, confirms the date is free, and submits “Request to book · Sat 14 Nov”. It follows the page theme and is sticky from 992px.

Use these only for searching and requesting a booking. Longer flows (paying the deposit, editing a request) use the regular form layout.

Anatomy

  1. Title.stub__title (an <h2> in --text-h3) or .booking-bar__title (--text-h4). It labels the form.
  2. Ticket code.stub__code or the bar’s <small> “Admit one church”: mono stamp, muted, aria-hidden while it is decoration.
  3. Perforation2px dashed rule under the head, in --color-border-strong.
  4. Price.stub__price in --text-figure-lg with a muted line on what it covers. Stub only.
  5. FieldsStandard .fields. The date’s help is an inline message confirming availability.
  6. SubmitPrimary, large. Full width in the stub; full width across both columns in the bar.
  7. Fine print.stub__fine: caption, muted. What is charged and when, and the cancellation rule.
  8. Ticket framePaper surface, 2px charcoal frame, 24px padding, hard 8px shadow: ink (light) or yellow (dark) on the stub, always yellow on the bar.

Auth card (“Admit one”)

Sign-in, sign-up, password reset, email confirmation and two-step codes are the same paper stub as the booking stub, centred on the stage in an auth layout. .auth-card on the .stub caps it at --layout-auth-width (28rem) and swaps the ink shadow for a yellow one so it reads on charcoal. The head stacks the mono kicker (“Admit one · Your account”) over the h1 in .auth-card__title; .auth-card__links spreads the secondary links (“Forgot your password?”, “Create an account”) under the full-width submit.

Sizes

Each ticket has one size. The bar fills its poster column; the stub fills the 22rem profile sidebar. Fields use the 44px control height and the submit the 56px large button.

  • Busy (booking bar only): the submit gets aria-busy="true" and aria-disabled="true" (the button’s busy pattern, never native disabled), a spinner and a present-tense label (“Checking calendars…”). It ignores further clicks so a request is never sent twice. The form gets aria-busy="true" too. Fields stay editable.
  • Invalid date: the date input gets aria-invalid="true" and a .field__error linked with aria-describedby. The message replaces the availability help; it does not sit beside it.
  • Booked date (stub): when the artist is not free on the chosen date, the help becomes the error “{first name} is booked {date}. Try another date.” and the submit is disabled (L2-019).
  • Hand-over (stub): the stub has no busy or sent state. “Request to book” opens the request page (/artists/{slug}/book) with the date, kind of gathering, church and message carried; the sending and sent states live there and on the booking page, where the booking number (No. ZAM-0114) first appears.
  • Available (default on the stub): the date help is a success inline message with a check icon, not a typed “✓”.

Responsive behaviour

  • Bar: one column of fields on phones. From --layout-breakpoint-sm (576px) it becomes two columns; the title and the submit span both. In the poster it stacks under the copy until 992px, then takes the right column.
  • Stub: under 992px it follows the reviews at the end of the profile, full width, and “Book for Sat 14 Nov” in the poster jumps to it. From 992px it sits in the --layout-sidebar-width (22rem) column and stays pinned (position: sticky) at --layout-topbar-height plus 24px while the profile scrolls.
  • The stub must fit a laptop screen without scrolling inside itself. Keep it to four fields; the message is optional and short.
  • The submit is always full width in the stub. A label that does not fit wraps onto two balanced lines instead of overflowing; in the 22rem sidebar “Request to book · Sat 14 Nov” already takes two, so it is the longest allowed.
  • Auth card: full width minus the page margins on phones, at most 28rem from there up, always centred; its links wrap onto two lines rather than shrinking.

Theming

Both tickets read --color-bg-surface, --color-fg-default and --color-border-strong. The difference is where their tokens come from:

  • The bar carries data-theme="light". Tokens re-declare the newsprint theme on that element, so the bar, its fields, its focus rings and its errors stay paper and ink in the dark theme, and the date picker uses a light color-scheme. Its shadow is --color-accent-on-stage, so it glows yellow against the stage in both themes.
  • The stub has no theme attribute. In light it is paper with an ink --shadow-3; in dark it is a charcoal surface with a light frame and a yellow --shadow-3.

The tickets have no component tokens of their own. Fields and buttons inside keep theirs (--field-*, --btn-*).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <form> named by its title with aria-labelledby, which makes it a form landmark (“Your event”, “Book Abigail”). The stub sits in an <aside> labelled by the same title. No APG widget pattern applies: every control is native. The date is <input type="date">; the date picker page covers its keyboard model.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the fields and the submit in reading order: date, kind, place or church, radius or message, submit.
EnterSubmits the form from any single-line field or the submit. In the message textarea it adds a new line.
SpaceActivates the focused submit.
↑ / ↓Change the focused part of the date, or the option in a closed select.

Focus

Fields show the double ring (--shadow-focus): yellow inside, ink outside on paper. On submit with errors, focus moves to the first invalid field. “Book for Sat 14 Nov” (or “Check dates”) in the poster scrolls to the stub and moves focus to its date field (L2-019, L2-012).

Inside the bar the button ring must be ink, not the stage yellow: the bar sits inside .on-stage, whose rule turns rings yellow, and yellow on paper is only 1.4:1. The paper island needs its own ring colour (see the note in Sources).

Labelling

Every control has a visible label. In the stub, use <label for> and keep help and error text outside the label, linked with aria-describedby, so the name stays “Event date” and the help is read as a description. The optional message says “(optional)” after its label, in .field__optional. “Admit one church” and the artist’s ticket code are aria-hidden. The busy booking-bar submit keeps its accessible name updated (“Checking calendars…”).

Motion

Only the busy spinner moves (--duration-loop); it keeps turning under reduced motion because it reports status. Field borders and focus rings change in --duration-fast, which drops to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Fields are 44px tall, the submit 56px, with 16–20px between fields. Native date and select pickers open on tap.

Content

  • Titles: “Your event” on the bar; “Book {first name}” on the stub.
  • Labels: short nouns or a plain question (“How far can they drive?”). Radius options pair distance and time: “120 km · 1.5 hr”.
  • Price: “From $650” and, below, what it covers: “per service · travel included”.
  • Availability: “Abigail is free Sat 14 Nov”. Errors say what happened and what to do: “Abigail is booked Sun 15 Nov. Try another date.” (L2-019).
  • Submit: verb plus date: “Request to book · Sat 14 Nov” (“Request to book” before a date is chosen), “Show the lineup”. Busy (booking bar): “Checking calendars…”.
  • Fine print: when money moves and the cancellation rule, in one or two sentences: “Nothing is charged until Abigail accepts. Cancel free up to 14 days before.”

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The bar read --palette-paper-bright and --palette-ink to stay light on the stage. It is now a data-theme="light" island that reads semantic tokens.
  • The stub heading used style="font: var(--text-h3)". It is now .stub__title. The stub price used an inline display-font shorthand; it now reads --text-figure-lg.
  • “✓ Abigail is free Sat 14 Nov” was muted help with a typed ✓. It is now .inline-msg--success with a check icon.
  • No field error existed. Invalid dates now use .field__error with aria-invalid and aria-describedby, and invalid styling covers inputs, selects and textareas.
  • Stub skeletons were sized inline. They now use .skeleton--title, --control, --control-lg.
  • Inside the poster, the .on-stage rule turned the submit’s focus ring yellow on the paper bar; components.css now restores the ink ring on a light island inside the stage.
