Date picker

The event date is the first thing a coordinator decides and the first field in every booking form. Zamaro uses the browser’s own date input, limited to today onward, with a few quick-pick dates beside it.

Overview

Use the date picker for the event date in the booking bar and the booking stub, and anywhere else a single calendar day is needed. It is a text field with type="date", inside a form field.

Why native

  • Phones get the system wheel or calendar, which coordinators already know, and the right keyboard on Android.
  • Keyboard and screen-reader support is built in: day, month and year segments, arrow keys, and announced values. A custom calendar grid has to follow the APG Date Picker Dialog example to match it, and most do not.
  • min stops past dates at the source: min="2026-10-09" greys out everything before today.
  • The value is always ISO (2026-11-14), whatever the display format, so there is no parsing of “14/11” versus “11/14”.
  • No script, no weight: nothing to load before the booking bar works.

The trade-offs are real. The display format follows the browser’s locale (“2026-11-14” or “11/14/2026”), so we repeat the friendly date in the message under the field (“Abigail is free Sat 14 Nov”). And the native calendar cannot mark which days an artist is free, so availability lives next to it: in quick-pick chips, in the tour dates list on the profile, and in the message after a date is chosen.

For a multi-day retreat use two date fields, “First day” and “Last day”, side by side in a form row. Do not build a range picker.

Anatomy

  1. Label“Event date”. Always visible.
  2. Date segmentsDay, month and year in the browser’s order, with tabular figures so the width does not jump.
  3. Calendar buttonDrawn by the browser. Opens the system calendar; on phones the whole field opens it.
  4. Availability messageAn inline message that repeats the date in words and says whether the artist is free.

Sizes

The date picker takes the text field sizes. Medium everywhere in the booking flow; large only if the date is alone on a screen.

36, 44 and 56px. Small is 14px text and makes iPhones zoom, so it is for wide screens only.
HTML
<input class="input input--sm" type="date" value="2026-11-14" aria-label="Small example">
<input class="input" type="date" value="2026-11-14" aria-label="Medium example">
<input class="input input--lg" type="date" value="2026-11-14" aria-label="Large example">

Both themes

  • Invalid covers a past date typed by hand, an empty required date, or a date the artist is booked. The error always offers the nearest free dates.
  • Read-only shows the date on a sent request.
  • Open is the system calendar, which follows color-scheme: light on newsprint, dark on the stage theme.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its field. In the booking bar it sits beside “Kind of gathering” from 576px and stacks above it on phones.
  • On phones, tapping anywhere in the field opens the system picker; the calendar button is a desktop affordance.
  • Quick-pick chips wrap onto a second line rather than scrolling sideways.

Theming

The date input is a .input and reads the same component tokens: --field-bg, --field-fg, --field-border, --field-height. It adds tabular figures. The calendar icon and popup are the browser’s and follow color-scheme, which the token sheet sets per theme. Preset chips use the chip tokens (--chip-bg, --chip-fg, --chip-border).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input type="date">; the browser exposes each segment as a spin button. If a custom calendar is ever needed (to mark free days inside it), follow the APG Date Picker Dialog example and keep the typed input. Quick-pick chips are toggle buttons in a labelled role="group".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves into the field (first segment) and out of it; in some browsers between segments too.
← / →Moves between day, month and year.
↑ / ↓Changes the focused segment.
DigitsType the segment directly.
Alt+↓ or SpaceOpens the calendar (Chrome, Edge).
Enter / Space on a chipFills the date. Focus stays on the chip.

Focus

The double ring around the whole field; the browser highlights the active segment inside it. Pressing a chip does not move focus into the field, so keyboard users can try several dates quickly.

Labelling

A visible label. The availability message is linked with aria-describedby and updated after a change; put it in a polite live region too, so “Abigail is booked Sun 15 Nov” is heard without hunting for it. The chip group is labelled “Quick dates for Event date”, and each chip’s text is a full date (“Sat 14 Nov”), not “Next”.

Motion

None of ours. The system calendar animates as the platform does.

Touch

The field is 44px tall; small chips are 36px with 8px gaps. The system picker on phones is designed for thumbs.

Content

  • Label: “Event date”. For two dates, “First day” and “Last day”.
  • Outside the field, write dates as Zamaro does: “Sat 14 Nov”, adding the year only when it is not this year or next (“Fri 5 Mar 2027”).
  • Chips name the day plainly: “This Sunday · 11 Oct”, “Sat 14 Nov”, “Christmas Eve”. Three or four at most.
  • Errors give the way out: “Abigail is booked Sun 15 Nov. Pick Sat 14 or Fri 20 Nov.” For a past date: “Pick a date from today, Fri 9 Oct.”

Extracted from these mocks:

  • pages/discover/empty: Christmas Eve (2026-12-24) with nobody free, and the nearby-date buttons that suggested quick picks

  • pages/artist/empty: Miriam Haile’s stub date, empty because no search date was carried

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The availability help was muted text with a typed ✓, inside the label. It is now an .inline-msg--success with an icon, linked by aria-describedby.
  • No booked-date error existed. .field__error with aria-invalid now covers it, and offers the nearest free dates.
  • Quick-pick chips are not in the mocks yet; they are part of the core set, drawn from the empty state’s nearby dates.
