Component · Navigation

Sidebar navigation

A vertical list of links for moving between the sections of an account area: an artist’s requests, bookings, calendar and payouts, or a church’s bookings and saved artists.

Overview

Use the sidebar when a signed-in area has four or more sections that people move between often. The artist dashboard is the main case: Abigail Mensah checks new requests, confirms bookings, blocks dates on her calendar and follows payouts. A booker’s account has fewer sections (requests, bookings, saved artists) and uses the same component with one group.

  • It is site navigation: every item is a link to a page, and the current page has aria-current="page".
  • Related links sit in groups. A group is a native <details>, so it can be folded away without JavaScript.
  • A yellow count badge shows how many items need the person’s attention, such as two new requests.

Do not use it on public pages (Discover, artist profiles): those use the top bar only. For two or three views of the same list, such as “Requests · Upcoming · Past”, use tabs instead.

Anatomy

  1. Group headingA <summary> in the mono overline style, muted, 24px minimum height.
  2. Fold marker“+” when closed, “–” when open. Drawn by CSS; the native marker is hidden.
  3. Current linkaria-current="page": yellow highlight, ink text and a 4px ink bar on the left.
  4. Icon and label20px stroke icon (aria-hidden) and a bold 14px label in .sidenav__label.
  5. Count badge (optional).badge--count, pushed to the right edge. Visually hidden text says what it counts.
  6. Panel.sidenav: surface fill, 2px charcoal rule, 16px padding, up to 18rem wide.

The sidebar has one size: 44px links in a panel up to 18rem wide. The collapsed rail is a variant, not a size.

  • Hover fills the row with --color-bg-subtle. Sidebar links do not lift; they are a list, not cards.
  • Current uses three cues at once: the yellow highlight, the 4px ink bar on the left and aria-current. It stays a working link.
  • Focus is the shared ring (ink on newsprint, yellow on the stage theme), drawn outside the row.
  • Closed group: its links are not rendered and are skipped by Tab. The current page’s group is never closed on load.

Responsive behaviour

  • 1280px and wider: the full sidebar sits in the first column of a two-column page layout, beside the content.
  • 1024–1279px: pages that need width (the artist’s calendar) use .sidenav--collapsed, a 76px rail. Others keep the full sidebar.
  • Below 1024px: the sidebar leaves the layout. Its links move into the top bar’s menu drawer, under the primary links and with the same groups, so there is one menu button on the page, not two.
  • The sidebar never scrolls sideways. Long labels wrap onto a second line inside the 44px-minimum row.

Theming

The sidebar is a paper surface, not a stage surface, so it follows the theme. It reads --color-bg-surface and --color-border-strong for the panel, --color-fg-default for links, --color-fg-muted for group headings, --color-bg-subtle for hover, and --color-accent-subtle, --color-fg-accent and --color-border-on-accent for the current link.

In the dark theme --color-fg-accent turns yellow and the highlight darkens, so the current link reads as yellow text on a dim amber fill. The left bar reads --color-border-on-accent, which stays ink in the dark theme and almost disappears there; the fill, the yellow text and aria-current still mark the page. A proposed fix draws the bar in --color-fg-accent (ink on newsprint, yellow on the stage theme). The count badge keeps its yellow fill and ink number in both themes. There are no --sidenav-* component tokens yet; to re-skin the current marker, override the three semantic tokens on the .sidenav element.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav> landmark with its own aria-label (“Artist dashboard”, “Your account”), containing lists of links. Groups are native <details>/<summary> disclosures, which follow the APG Disclosure pattern without extra ARIA. It is not a role="tree" or role="menu": those promise arrow-key behaviour that site navigation does not need.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through group headings and the links of open groups, top to bottom.
EnterFollows a link; opens or closes a group when its heading has focus.
SpaceOpens or closes a group when its heading has focus.

Focus

The shared 3px ring with a 3px offset, outside the row so it is not hidden by the current-page highlight. After a link loads a new page, focus goes to the page’s <h1> (or main), not back to the sidebar.

Labelling

Each sidebar <nav> has a unique name so screen-reader users can tell it from the “Primary” nav in the top bar. Counts carry visually hidden words (“2 new”, “3 artists”), so a link reads “Requests 2 new”. In the collapsed rail the visible label is gone, so the label text stays in the DOM (clipped, not display: none) and the count moves into it.

Motion

Nothing animates. Groups open and close instantly (native <details>), and the hover fill changes without a transition.

Touch

Links are 44px tall (--target-comfortable) and full width. Group headings are at least 24px tall and span the panel. In the collapsed rail each icon target is 44 × 44px.

Content

  • Labels are nouns for the pages they open, one or two words: “Requests”, “Bookings”, “Saved artists”, “Calendar”, “Payouts”.
  • Group headings are short and specific: “Gigs”, “Money”, “Profile”, or the church’s name in a booker account.
  • Keep the order stable: daily work first (requests, bookings, calendar), money next, profile and settings last.
  • Counts mean “needs your attention”: new requests, requests awaiting a reply. Do not count things that need nothing, like past bookings.

Sources

Not in the mocks yet; part of the core set. The eight mock pages are public (Discover and the artist profile), and none of them has an account area. The product will need it for:

  • The artist dashboard: requests, bookings, calendar, payouts and profile editing.
  • The booker account: Naomi’s requests, confirmed bookings and the saved artists that the top-bar “Saved (3)” and the footer link in pages/discover/default point to.

Drift fixed during extraction: none, since no mock uses it. The current-link bar uses --color-border-on-accent from the start rather than the ink primitive the mocks used elsewhere, and the count badge uses the corrected 1.5em minimum width.
