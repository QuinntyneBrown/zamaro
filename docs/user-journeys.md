# User journeys

| # | Name | Description | Effort | Complete |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find an artist | Search by date and place, sort and filter results, and see alternatives when everyone is booked. | 12 days | 100% |
| 2 | Choose an artist | Read an artist's profile, reviews and rating, share it, and pick a free date to book. | 21 days | 96% |
| 3 | Request a booking | Send a booking request, track it, message the artist, and withdraw it if plans change. | 16 days | 0% |
| 4 | Respond to a request | Accept or decline a booking request and follow the booking through to the event. | 13 days | 0% |
| 5 | Pay for a booking | Pay the deposit and the balance online and receive a receipt for each payment. | 21 days | 0% |
| 6 | Get paid | Receive the payout for a completed booking, less the platform fee. | 5 days | 0% |
| 7 | Manage availability | Mark free and busy dates, and publish a calendar feed to a personal calendar. | 10 days | 0% |
| 8 | Become an artist | Apply as an artist, upload a Vulnerable Sector Check, and be approved by the Zamaro team. | 19 days | 93% |
| 9 | Join and sign in | Create a church account, sign in, recover access, and manage church and account settings. | 23 days | 92% |
| 10 | Build a profile | Edit profile details, address and setlist, and upload photos and videos from the artist dashboard. | 27 days | 0% |
| 11 | Stay informed | Receive emails and event reminders about bookings, and choose which emails to get. | 11 days | 16% |
| 12 | Cancel a booking | Cancel a booking and receive the refund the cancellation policy allows. | 6 days | 0% |
| 13 | Review an artist | Review an artist after the event; artists reply, and anyone can report a review. | 13 days | 0% |
| 14 | Save artists | Save artists to a shortlist and come back to them later. | 5 days | 100% |
| 15 | Control my data | Export personal data, delete the account, and keep private details out of public view. | 11 days | 25% |
| 16 | Administer the platform | Administrators sign in securely, support bookings and payments, suspend artists, and audit actions. | 22 days | 44% |
| 17 | Use a fast, secure site | Fast, secure, accessible pages that work on any screen, in light or dark theme. | 72 days | 53% |

## Why this order

1. **Journeys 1–6** are the marketplace itself: a church finds a free artist, books, pays, and the artist
   is paid. Without them Zamaro adds nothing a phone call doesn't.
2. **Journeys 7–10** make bookings trustworthy and keep artists coming. Accurate calendars stop
   double-bookings. Vetting protects churches. Accounts and rich profiles are what let a church choose.
3. **Journeys 11–13** make an existing booking less work: fewer surprises, a fair way out, and
   reputations other churches can rely on.
4. **Journeys 14–16** are conveniences, user rights, and the team's operating tools. They matter, but
   bookers and artists rarely see them.
5. **Journey 17** has no value of its own, but every other journey depends on it. Build it first even
   though it ranks last on value (see the [roadmap](estimates/README.md#roadmap)).
