How booking works

Four steps. Nothing is charged until an artist says yes.

  1. 01

    Pick your date

    The calendar counts who is free each day within your drive distance. Select a day to see each artist’s open dates around it.

  2. 02

    Send a request

    Choose an artist, add your service time, venue and what you hope the worship will carry. It takes about two minutes.

  3. 03

    Hear back within 48 hours

    The artist accepts, suggests a change or kindly declines. We hold the date for you while they decide.

  4. 04

    Confirm with a deposit

    A 25% deposit locks the date; the rest is paid after the service. Free cancellation up to 30 days before.

Do you lead worship?

Keep your open dates on Zamaro and churches within driving distance will find you on the days you’re free. Joining is free; we take 8% only when a booking is confirmed.
