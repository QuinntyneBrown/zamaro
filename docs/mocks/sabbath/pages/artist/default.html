About Abigail

Abigail grew up singing in the choir of her family’s Ghanaian Pentecostal church in Brampton, and she still leads there twice a month. She brings a warm alto, a keyboard and a quiet confidence that helps a room stop performing and start praying.

She leads in English and Twi, moves easily between gospel standards, hymns and newer congregational songs, and is happy to teach a new song to your team at soundcheck. Most churches book her for worship nights, anniversaries and Sunday services with a guest focus.
