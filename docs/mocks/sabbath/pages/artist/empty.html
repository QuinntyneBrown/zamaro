About Miriam

Miriam leads from the piano with a clear soprano, and she loves the old hymns most of all. She has served on the worship team at her Ethiopian Evangelical church in Etobicoke for eleven years and joined Zamaro in September 2026.

She is at home in small rooms: midweek prayer meetings, seniors’ services, retreats and Sunday mornings where the hymnal is still open. She can lead in English and Amharic.
