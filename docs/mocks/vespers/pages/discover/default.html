How booking works

No back-and-forth emails. Your deposit is held until the artist has played.

  1. Find who’s free

    Search by date and place. Only artists open that day and willing to travel to you appear.

  2. Send a request

    Share your service order, set times and sound setup. Artists reply within 24 hours, usually much sooner.

  3. Confirm and worship

    Pay a 25% deposit to lock the date. The rest is released the day after your gathering.
