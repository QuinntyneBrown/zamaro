About Abigail

I grew up in the front row of my father’s church in Brampton, learning harmonies from aunties who never needed a microphone. Nine years ago I started leading worship at New Covenant Chapel, and since then I’ve sung at Sunday services, revival nights and more weddings than I can count.

My heart is to help a room stop performing and start praying. I bring a tight two-piece band (keys and percussion) or come alone with tracks, and I’ll gladly learn your congregation’s favourites ahead of the day.
