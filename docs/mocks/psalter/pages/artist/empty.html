I. Her story

I learned to play on the upright piano at Bethany Ethiopian Evangelical Church in Etobicoke, where I still lead the Wednesday prayer service. My worship is quiet: I would rather leave space for a congregation to hear its own voice than fill every moment.

I am new to Zamaro this autumn and would be honoured to serve your church, whether that is a Sunday morning, a prayer vigil or a small memorial.
