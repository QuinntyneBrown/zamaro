I. Her story

I grew up in the front row of my father’s church in Brampton, learning harmonies from aunties who never needed a microphone. Nine years ago I began leading worship at New Covenant Chapel, and since then I have sung at Sunday services, revival nights, weddings and more than a few farewells.

I come prepared to serve your congregation, not to perform for it. Send me the songs your people love, and I will bring a few they have not met yet, in English and in Twi. I lead from the keyboard or with your own musicians, and I am glad to rehearse with your team the week before.
