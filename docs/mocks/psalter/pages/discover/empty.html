How booking works

  1. Choose your date

    See only the artists who are free that day, within driving distance of your church.

  2. Send a request

    Tell them about the service: the time, the congregation, the songs you love. Nothing is charged yet.

  3. Hear back within two days

    The artist accepts, asks a question, or suggests another time.

  4. Confirm with a deposit

    A 25% deposit holds the date. We keep it safe until after the service.

  5. Worship, then reflect

    The balance is paid after the service, and your review helps the next church.
