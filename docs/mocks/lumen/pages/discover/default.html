How booking works

From your first search to the last song, in four steps. You never pay anything until an artist has said yes.

  1. Pick your date

    Tell us the day and where your church is. We only show artists who are free then and happy to make the drive.

  2. Send a request

    Choose an artist and share your service details. A request is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.

  3. They confirm

    Artists reply within 48 hours. Once they accept, a 25% deposit holds your date; the rest is due a week before.

  4. Worship together

    Plan the set list together in Zamaro messages. Afterwards, leave a review to help the next church.
