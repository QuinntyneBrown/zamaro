About Abigail

Abigail grew up singing in her grandmother’s church choir in Kumasi, Ghana, and has led worship in the Greater Toronto Area for twelve years. She is at home with a full gospel band, a single piano or a congregation singing a cappella.

She loves helping churches blend the songs they already know with new ones, and she’ll gladly learn your hymns ahead of time. Most Sundays you’ll find her at New Covenant Tabernacle in Brampton.
