About Miriam

Miriam studied piano at the Royal Conservatory and has led worship at her Ethiopian Evangelical church in Etobicoke since she was sixteen. She leads from the keys and is happy to work alone or alongside your musicians.

She’s new to Zamaro this month and is looking forward to serving churches across the west end and Halton.
