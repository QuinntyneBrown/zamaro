About

Raised in the choir loft

I grew up in my mother’s church in Brampton, learning harmonies from aunties who never needed a microphone. I’ve led worship at New Covenant Chapel for nine years.

I lead in English and Twi, bring my own tracks if you don’t have a band, and I’m happy to rehearse with your volunteers. My aim is a congregation that sings.
