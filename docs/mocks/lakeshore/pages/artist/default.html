About Abigail

I grew up in the front row of my father’s church in Brampton, learning harmonies from aunties who never needed a microphone. For nine years I’ve led worship at New Covenant Chapel, and I’ve sung at Sunday services, revival nights and more weddings than I can count.

My heart is to help a room stop performing and start praying. I can come alone with tracks or bring keys and percussion, and I’ll happily learn your congregation’s favourites before the day.
