How booking works

Three steps, no back-and-forth emails. Your deposit is held safely until the artist has led your service.

  1. 1

    Pick your date and place

    Only artists who are free that day and willing to drive to your church show up on the map, with the drive time from your door.

  2. 2

    Send a request

    Share your order of service, set length and sound setup. Artists reply within 24 hours, and most reply the same day.

  3. 3

    Confirm and worship

    Pay a 25% deposit to hold the date. Zamaro releases the rest to the artist the day after your service.

  • Free cancellation up to 14 days before
  • Every artist’s identity and references checked
  • Travel fees shown up front, before you request
